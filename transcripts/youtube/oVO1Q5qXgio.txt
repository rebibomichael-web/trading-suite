You tokenize something for two reasons. One is to actually make it more freely accessible to a global audience, ideally in a 24/7 way, right? That we've already seen that with stable coins. Stable coins made dollars very accessible for a global audience because to John's point, anyone can kind of access it now 24/7 on public blockchain rails and hold it in a self-custodial wallet. Even if you do not trust your local regulators, your local banks, your local whatever, as we recently saw in some Latin American jurisdictions, you can actually access these dollars whenever you want.
