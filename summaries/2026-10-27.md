**The AI Correction, Tech Earnings and the Rally – 10/27/26**

**Overview:** The AI trade is bouncing hard, helped by falling rates, narrower credit spreads and cleaner positioning. The Nasdaq was up about 1.3% going into big-tech earnings from Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon. The panel agreed the setup looks better but disagreed on how much funding risk remains, especially at the neoclouds.

- **Breaking news (Seema Mody):** Oracle is adding OpenAI tools, including ChatGPT, to its application stack. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure is also adding more accelerators, including Google TPUs, OpenAI's "Jalapeno" chip and Cerebras. Oracle says this is not a move away from Nvidia. Oracle was up about 4%. Sam Altman was due on stage at Oracle AI World.
- **Joe Terranova:**
  - He likes the setup for a year-end return of optimism and wants to focus on large caps.
  - He wants good news to get good price reactions. He disliked the reactions to Western Digital, Lam and Micron.
  - Today's reaction is what he wants: Intel up 4%, Cerebras up 8%, Nvidia rallying.
  - He is bullish on momentum into year-end. Momentum is coming off its best day since Sept 21, and he ties it to the rate of change in yields.
  - Semis: after Cadence's quarter, he likes Synopsys (reports in December) and Teradyne (reports tomorrow). Neither is in his ETF.
  - On Brazil, he prefers picking individual companies over the broad market.
- **Stephanie Link:**
  - We are in the "fourth inning" of AI, the third of cybersecurity and the first of robotics. She says those are her three favorite themes.
  - The 10-year yield is down about 42 bps.
  - Tech earnings growth is running at about 200% and communication services at 48%, with only about 20% of tech reported.
  - Broadcom (up about 4%):
    - D.A. Davidson upgraded it to Buy and raised its target to $400 from $350.
    - The Anthropic deal secures about $60B of 2027 revenue.
    - Broadcom has guided AI revenue to $115B in 2027 and $230B in 2028, with gross margin of 73% and operating margin of 66%.
    - She sees about $22 of 2028 earnings power, which puts the stock at about 15x. It is down 2% on the year. She also owns Nvidia.
  - SpaceX: she holds it long term and would nibble on pullbacks. AI compute rental grew over 700%, connectivity grew 70%, and she sees about 200M Starlink subscribers by 2030. Bank of America added it to its US 1 list.
  - Brazil (EWZ): she added after it fell about 25% on the election result. Her case rests on demographics (214M people), agriculture at 23% of GDP, power, a stronger currency and falling inflation. She called the drop overdone.
  - UPS beat and raised guidance for the second quarter in a row. It trades at 12.5x earnings, yields 6.5% and is up 8.5% on the year.
  - Boeing fell about 10%. It beat on revenue in defense and services, but costs and supply chain were worse and the $1–3B free cash flow target looks hard to reach. She is unsure what to do with it and prefers GE, which she added to after the stock fell on earnings.
  - She recently bought Zscaler, which is up only 15% on the year against over 100% for Palo Alto and CrowdStrike. She cites a 50% discount to peers on EV/sales, RPO growth of 27% and ARR growth of 25%.
  - She expects the industrial reports (Quanta, GE Vernova, Vertiv) to be good.
- **Josh Brown:**
  - He loves the setup. He says he asked two weeks ago what happens when rates back off, and rallies follow relief on rates and oil.
  - Valuations: Sandisk has a PEG of 0.08, Micron 0.10 and Western Digital 0.13. Nvidia trades at about 15x forward earnings.
  - He expects Amazon's Thursday report to be potentially "explosive".
  - CrowdStrike's George Kurtz laid out a plan for $10B ARR by 2030 (a year early) and potentially $20B by the mid-2030s.
  - **Best Stocks in the Market:**
    - Meta is off the list. He was stopped out at $685 on Oct 22 and is not re-entering. He wants it to retake its 50-day, about $664.
    - Cloudflare (NET) stays on the list.
      - Non-human traffic is over 50% of its network, and AI agent requests are up 1700% year over year.
      - It reports Nov 5 after the close, and the trader stop is $299.
    - He says cyber is strong and broad: CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, Fortinet, and Okta as a takeover target.
  - He added the Alphabet convertible preferred to play the traditional hyperscalers.
- **Jenny Harrington:**
  - She is the cautious voice. She says neocloud funding has dried up, and that Broadcom CEO Hock Tan having to step in on Anthropic's financing says something fundamental has changed.
  - She sees a "pocket of toxicity" and prefers steadier cash-flow growers.
  - Low PEGs reflect skepticism about growth, and a blowout from Amazon could still get a muted reaction.
  - She does see the AI economic activity broadening.
  - Steph, Joe and Josh countered that the market is already discriminating: capital is going to strong balance sheets (Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Broadcom) and away from CoreWeave and the neoclouds.
- **Mike Santoli (Market Memo):**
  - He says it is a "now or never" moment for the AI bounce. Hedge fund tech exposure and leverage are way down, so a tactical bounce is warranted.
  - He wants the semiconductor ETF (SMH) to reclaim its 20- and 50-day moving averages, about 5% higher.
  - He says financing concerns are not fully cleared. Meta priced its deal at 625 against 772 two weeks ago, and SpaceX debt and Oracle trade like junk despite investment-grade ratings.
- **Options Action (Oliver Renick):**
  - Alphabet: stock is finding support at $320, with 170K calls against 70K puts. A big trader sold about $8M of the Nov 20 337.5 calls. The implied earnings move is 5.5%.
  - Microsoft: volume is very low, and some call buying reversed as sellers hit the $500–600 calls. He calls it neutral.
- **Other:** Oracle's earnings were $1.92 against $1.74 expected, and Western Digital's were $4.64 against $4.11. Micron trades at about 5x earnings against a historical 10x. Terranova noted it is still up about 220% on the year.
- **Final Trades:**
  - **Josh Brown:** Delta. If oil comes down, it will "act like it's on a trampoline." It absorbed $6B in fuel costs this year.
  - **Jenny Harrington:** American Tower, UnitedHealth, Apple.
  - Joe Terranova's and Stephanie Link's final trades were not captured in the transcript.
