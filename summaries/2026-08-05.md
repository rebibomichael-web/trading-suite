Stocks Extend Record Highs: The Investment Committee's Strategy — August 5, 2026

**Overview:** The Investment Committee debated whether the S&P 500's record run has room to run toward 8,000 (and even Ed Yardeni's 8,250 target), largely hinging on whether the broadening earnings story outside of Big Tech continues or the market re-concentrates into momentum names. Individual stock stories dominated the second half — Alphabet leadership shake-up, SpaceX's first earnings as a public-ish issuer, Disney's "good numbers, bad stock" conundrum, and a new activist stake in Shake Shack from Starboard's Jeff Smith.

- **Joe Terranova**: Warned the real risk is the market re-concentrating into names like Nvidia, Micron, Marvell and Corning rather than sustaining the rotation into "the other 493" (energy, industrials); noted his JOET momentum ETF is at all-time highs but the math to 8,250 doesn't require much more from momentum since top-10 S&P names are still ~5% below their own highs even after a 5% market move; called Alphabet's selloff an overreaction and reiterated Nvidia is a buy right here, "a hanging curveball down the middle," ~19-20x forward earnings vs ~30-35% EPS growth.
- **Liz Thomas (filling in as "macro" voice)**: Said it's hard to be pessimistic — 73% of S&P above 200-day average, ~90% of financials above their 200-day average confirming cyclicality — but flagged risk that the sharp momentum rebound (semis were down ~30% before this bounce) could cool off; expects a rotation back to large caps over small caps in H2.
- **Jason Snipe**: Cited 29% blended earnings growth, 65% of S&P reported, 85% beat rate as very strong; said hitting Yardeni's 8,250 requires momentum to keep participating; on Alphabet, called the selloff a knee-jerk reaction given 82% Google Cloud revenue growth and said one departure (chief scientist Jeff Dean after 27 years) doesn't undercut the broader AI bench; on Uber, flagged the lack of a near-term catalyst as his concern despite $10B free cash flow.
- **Jim Lebenthal ("Farmer Jim")**: Argued ISM manufacturing/services strength historically correlates with broadening earnings; on Disney, said operating results (parks, streaming, studios, box office recovery) are strong but the stock (~14x forward earnings) isn't reflecting it — called it a market that wants to "get behind the underdog," pointed to Microsoft/Palantir-style re-rating potential and floated M&A/spinoff jitters (cable network spinoffs) as a possible overhang; on AMD's ~6% drop despite a good quarter, blamed Elon Musk's on-air Nvidia endorsement stealing the spotlight and noted AMD's mid-40s forward multiple vs Nvidia's ~19.5x.
- **Alphabet/DeepMind news (Mackenzie Segalos)**: Google shares down ~3.5% (later ~5% intraday) after Demis Hassabis moves to a chairman role focused on frontier research/AGI safety, Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over day-to-day DeepMind leadership, and chief scientist Jeff Dean departs after ~27 years to start a new company Google plans to invest in — context includes recent departures of John Jumper (to Anthropic) and Noam Shazir (to OpenAI).
- **Kevin Simpson (guest, SpaceX shareholder)**: Called the SpaceX quarter operationally "fantastic" — revenue up 92% to $7.8B, Starlink revenue up 66%, AI-related revenue up 247% — but said enthusiasm for the stock is tempered by tomorrow's share lockup expiration and coming supply increase; hasn't added to his position (a 0.5% stake via the QDVO growth ETF at $160), calling it a multi-year story on AI/Starship monetization.
- **SpaceX stock reaction**: Shares down ~9-10% on soaring AI costs ahead of the lockup; mixed analyst target revisions (several cuts, a couple of raises).
- **Nvidia**: Up nearly 4%, the only green mega-cap, boosted by Elon Musk calling it "the best AI computer" on the SpaceX earnings call; Mellius (analyst) positive on the endorsement; Terranova reiterated it's a buy ~6-7% from an all-time high.
- **Shake Shack activist news (Brandon Gomez)**: Starboard's Jeff Smith disclosed a stake, calling shares "too cheap"; stock jumped as much as ~11.5%.
- **Josh Brown (phone-in guest)**: Long-term shareholder; said Shake Shack's real estate/site selection is "an A," digital/drive-thru execution is "a B," but the stock shocked the Street with a surprise loss last quarter (beef/paper cost spikes tied to Iran/oil disruption); expects Starboard to push operational fixes and likely land a board seat, calling Smith's quick-serve activist record (Darden, Papa John's) "unimpeachable."
- **Terranova on Starboard/Darden history**: Recalled Starboard replacing Darden's entire 12-member board in 2014 (stock +143% over the following five years) and Starboard's granular operational activism (e.g., changing Olive Garden's pasta-cooking process).
- **Private equity/Apollo**: Apollo reiterated overweight; Snipe noted record fee-related earnings of $785M (+25%), insurance spread income driving results, and a correlation between software strength and PE stock performance; wants to see more monetizations before adding.
- **Booking Holdings / consumer discretionary (Terranova, on his JOET rebalance)**: Booking up ~6% on earnings; Terranova recently exited Expedia (debt-to-equity spiked to ~500%, disqualifying it on his quality screen despite strong price momentum), Las Vegas Sands (price momentum), and Royal Caribbean (Middle East-related weakness) from his momentum ETF.
- **Options Action (Oliver Renick)**: Flagged a rare VIX-up/S&P-up dynamic this week — record 4M+ SPX calls traded Monday, a 40% single-day jump in NASDAQ near-the-money call prices (biggest in five years) — signaling strong bullish conviction but risk of "leakage" in overpriced calls; Terranova called it more a short-term high-frequency/algo mechanic than a signal about the future.
- **Uber**: Shares down ~7% on a revenue miss (~12% growth vs. ~14% expected) despite in-line EPS and $10B free cash flow; Snipe not in the name, citing no clear next catalyst despite long-term belief in the AV/mobility business model.
- **Final Trades**: Jim Lebenthal – Wynn Resorts (WYNN); Jason Snipe – Microsoft (MSFT); Liz Thomas – commodities as an inflation hedge; Joe Terranova – (Restaurant/AI theme reference, cut off) end-of-show wrap.
