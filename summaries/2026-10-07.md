**Halftime Report Celebrates 15 Years — 10/7/26**

**Overview:** The show marked its 15th anniversary with the S&P 500 near record highs (first close above 7,800 yesterday). The market was down on the day because the 10-year yield hit 5.31%, its highest since April 2002. The panel debated why stocks are so resilient despite rising yields, wars, and narrow breadth, and looked back at memorable moments, including the 2013 Ackman–Icahn clash. Committee members also gave fresh trades and Calls of the Day.

**Who said what:**

- **Josh Brown:** Cited his partner Michael Batnick's view that 2026 is "skepticism versus greed" rather than fear versus greed. The VIX is around 15, yet multiples keep contracting even as earnings set records, with NVIDIA as the example. He is not worried by the narrow market: with 40% of stocks below their 200-day average, there are things to buy that aren't at highs. He said he'd change his view if there were three bad jobs reports, a tightening Fed, and negative earnings revisions. He noted that since the first show the ETF market has grown from $954B to $16.3T, the US economy from $15T to $32T, and the US stock market from $11.9T to $32.6T. He also pointed out that someone sold a huge block of his JOT ETF live on air, and said he takes the other side. On Berkshire, he has never sold it and treats it like an ETF. Final trade: ServiceTitan, and he's staying long.
- **Stephanie Link:** Favors best-in-breed companies bought on sale. She says growth is running about 4–5% versus her expected 3%. Yields are rising on real yields, not inflation, since 5- and 10-year breakevens are at 2.4%. She'd worry if the 10-year reached about 6%. Her themes are AI infrastructure, cybersecurity ("bigger than AI"), automation/robotics, and animal and women's health. She bought NVIDIA in July near $200 at about 18x forward earnings, and added Meta a couple of weeks ago. Final trade: Muse, which she calls a game changer. She cited 5 million downloads in 22 days (ChatGPT took 56 days, Grok 103) and the #1 spot on Apple's App Store. The stock is flat over the past year and she sees room to catch up.
- **Joe Terranova:** Said the last 15 years have been "buy high, sell higher," driven by tech. The Nasdaq is up about 1,000%, and every top-10 company is now above $1T. Portfolio managers use a handful of mega-cap stocks (Microsoft, NVIDIA, Apple) as both offense and defense. That is one reason the quality factor is lagging. Cyclicals need lower oil and yields to work.
- **Steve Weiss:** The S&P trades at 19.3x versus 23x a year ago, because earnings growth is the strongest in decades. Over the past month every sector except tech is down and energy is flat. He thinks defense stocks are trading at lows and AMD's multiple is unreasonable. He argued that index investors no longer sell dips. Final trade: SK Hynix, which he doesn't own. He likes what he sees in Micron and thinks it has more legs.
- **Brynne (Kelly), Shannon Saccocia, Jim Lebenthal, Jenny Harrington (A-block guests):**
  - **Brynne:** Holds RSP, which was breaking out six weeks ago. She said rates going from zero to "normal" are stressing the broader market. NVIDIA and Micron will supply a third of 2026 S&P earnings growth and 40% in 2027.
  - **Shannon Saccocia:** Broadening is at risk because of rate sensitivity and global yield volatility. Mega-cap tech has become a "safety trade" because of its free cash flow.
  - **Jim Lebenthal:** His diversified portfolio (industrials, materials, financials) has hurt for 4–6 weeks. He sees a multiple reset to pre-ZIRP levels, with earnings growth as the saving grace.
  - **Jenny Harrington:** Bought Alphabet convertible preferreds, as she described the day before. She runs a weekly screen for yields above 3.5%.
- **Calls of the Day:**
  - **Spotify:** B of A reiterated Buy with a $680 target. Link said the news has been good and the stock is caught in the media-stock vortex and is stabilizing.
  - **ServiceTitan:** Morgan Stanley is Overweight with a $92 target. Link said it has been thrown out with software and users on its AI platform are stickier.
  - **Coinbase:** Barclays has a $149 target (up from $95) but keeps a Sell. Link wants small crypto exposure and likes the shift toward subscriptions and services.
- **Kevin Simpson:** Added to SpaceX on a pullback. He initially bought near $121.21 and it is at its highest since June. It is rising on news that it is raising money to buy more NVIDIA chips. He sees it as a 15-year core holding, alongside Tesla.
- **Sarat (Sethi):** Added to Ferrari (limited supply, EV concerns) and Disney (12x earnings, new management, better products).
- **Bill Baruch:** Cut Berkshire in half, after buying it in June below $500, and used the proceeds to start a Morgan Stanley position. Berkshire is low beta and the market is breaking out. Morgan Stanley trades at 14x and is 20% off its high, and its wealth management AUM in alternatives grew 87% last quarter.
- **Anniversary moments:** The panel recalled the January 25, 2013 Ackman–Icahn on-air clash, which ran about 22 minutes. They later reunited the two at a Delivering Alpha event. Wapner thanked the production staff and executive producer Kevin Flynn. A montage covered Brexit, COVID, tariffs, and guests including Shaq and Jensen Huang.

**Final Trades:**
- **Josh Brown:** Long ServiceTitan.
- **Stephanie Link:** Muse.
- **Steve Weiss:** SK Hynix and Micron.
