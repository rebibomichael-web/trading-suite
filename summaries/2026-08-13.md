**Navigating the Market at Record Highs: Your Next Trade — August 13, 2026**

**Overview:** Stocks stayed calm near record highs as a cooler PPI reading eased rate-hike worries and reinforced a "hold" bias at the Fed, while the panel argued the real story is blowout corporate earnings — record profit margins, pricing power, and AI capex that's now visibly paying off in hyperscaler cloud growth. Chip and AI-adjacent names had a wild week of double-digit swings, sparking debate over how much of the rally is disciplined institutional buying versus momentum chasing, while Bill Ackman's Pershing Square disclosed a new Netflix stake four years after dumping the stock at a loss.

- **Josh Brown**: Says the market keeps proving the bulls right — earnings are beating and raising across 10 of 11 S&P sectors, capex is accelerating, and there's no need for "Fed gossip." Long Netflix, argues it's cheap at 20x forward earnings given 42% expected earnings growth next year. On NVIDIA's underperformance vs. peers, cites multiple compression at a $5T cap vs. easier gains for smaller suppliers. Featured in **Best Stocks in the Market**: Ferguson Enterprises (FERG) — Jefferies PT $289; likes the HVAC/plumbing supplier's data-center industrial exposure and dual-contractor strategy; watching for a breakout above $250 after three failed attempts.
- **Stephanie Link**: Bought more Micron (MU), now down "only" 30% from highs (was 40%), citing $100B in take-or-pay bookings through decade-end, $22B cash, and ~$40 in earnings power. Bought Timken (TKR) as a new position — automation/robotics and rail/power-gen play under new "Elevate to Outperform" self-help strategy, targeting 11% revenue growth and 700bps gross-margin expansion by 2028. Added to Carrier (CTR) after trimming it earlier at $80. Keeps buying AST SpaceMobile (ASTS) on weakness despite the stock falling — cites 92% revenue growth, 191% EBITDA growth, $14B in AI-era commitments this quarter, sees connectivity business reaching $60B/year.
- **Jenny Harrington**: Admits she's "uncomfortably" bullish near-term, says the market's calm — no scary headlines since June — reflects real underlying strength and pricing power, not complacency. Sold Stanley Black & Decker (SWK) for a 45% total return (12.5% annualized) after her thesis played out, but is sitting on cash with no attractive entry points, needs a pullback and a 4%+ dividend yield to buy anything new. Losing (but hasn't yet lost) her burger bet with Josh Brown on Netflix vs. Disney total return — up 30% but says the gap should close since she still prefers Disney's diversification long-term.
- **Malcolm Etheridge**: Warns chip-stock trading has entered "crazy town," with investors piling into names like Cerebras and CoreWeave without understanding the underlying tech, driven by marginal/pod buyers rather than fundamentals. Owns Cerebras, wanted to add on a post-earnings pullback but the stock never dropped enough (net loss narrowing from ~30% to ~18% guided). On Ackman's new Netflix stake, questions the lift given decelerating subscriber growth, but figures the thesis is about monetizing the existing 300M-subscriber base rather than chasing new growth.
- **Featured segments**: Kate Rooney reported OpenAI's Chief Revenue Officer Denise Dresser is departing after less than a year (replaced by Dolly Rodgick, ex-Wiz CEO), part of a string of high-profile OpenAI exits, while Anthropic reportedly moves toward an IPO at a ~$2T valuation — panel discussed the scrutiny both AI labs face given they underpin ~50% of hyperscaler capex commitments. A segment revisited Bill Ackman's new 3M-share (4.9% of portfolio) Netflix stake, four years after Pershing Square exited at a $400M loss; Netflix shares rose ~3.5% on the news. Mike Santoli's Midday Word: the market absorbed last month's internal tensions without a real break thanks to strong Big Tech earnings, but he's watching for signs the market is "over-earning" and could give back gains.
- **Final Trades**: Malcolm Etheridge — Netflix (NFLX); Jenny Harrington — Cisco (CSCO), buy on weakness; Stephanie Link — Union Pacific (UNP); Josh Brown — CrowdStrike (CRWD), up 100% over the past 52 weeks.
