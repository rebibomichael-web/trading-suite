**Anthropic IPO Shakes AI Trade and Market Sentiment – Halftime Report, Nov. 19, 2026**

**Overview:** Mike Santoli hosted, filling in for Scott Wapner. The show covered Anthropic's record IPO and how it fits into a shaky market, with the S&P at a new cycle low, the VIX above 25, the Dow down about 1.6% and below 50,000, and chip stocks under pressure. The panel debated whether the deal helps or hurts sentiment, and what else to buy while waiting on the stock.

- **Anthropic IPO (Leslie Picker):**
  - Priced at $83, $5 above the range, with the offering upsized.
  - It raised $81B in the offering plus $26B in a concurrent private placement. An over-allotment would add about $12B, for a total near $119B, the largest IPO ever.
  - It opened at $111.50, hit a high of about $115 and a low of $95.05, and traded around $97 later (roughly 17–22% above the IPO price).
  - The fully diluted value was about $2T at the open, then back below that. SpaceX's IPO in June raised about $86B including the over-allotment.
- **Byron Dieter (early investor, at Nasdaq):**
  - Called it "the Google moment in 2004" for the AI supercycle.
  - Said the market is big enough for several winners (a "cocktail" of closed and open-weight models). He said hyperscalers need Anthropic to succeed and it's "not a winner-take-all dynamic."
  - Described the TAM as the internet and cloud supercycles combined.
- **Matt Murphy (Menlo Ventures, via David Faber):**
  - Invested starting in 2023 and led the Series D. His aggregate position is about $25B, possibly around 240M shares.
  - Menlo bought in the IPO and signed the extended lockup. He had no forward guidance, but noted Anthropic added about $10B of ARR in a month. Last ARR was about $81B.
  - Said Anthropic has over 55% enterprise share, and dismissed "regulatory capture" claims.
- **Ravi Mattrey (Lightspeed, via Faber):**
  - Led the Series E at a $61B valuation, when run-rate revenue was about $1B.
  - Said being public raises transparency and governance standards, and called the public benefit structure "the right structure."
  - Said safety work on alignment and interpretability will be critical for recursive self-improvement.
- **Bryn Talkington:**
  - Called it a great day for a new stock, and thinks it may replace Nvidia as the public's favorite AI plaything.
  - Said the lockup and liquidity cliff starts in Q1 and wondered how much VCs will actually sell.
  - Noted Anthropic spends about $1.03 per $1 of revenue, with about 58% going to compute.
  - Prefers to play the capex theme through SpaceX, Google and Amazon rather than chase Anthropic.
  - Said the new raise cements AI capex. Thinks Fed hawkishness (Waller) is "insane" given the housing and commercial real estate weakness, and flagged Elizabeth Warren's coming influence on bank policy.
  - Likes selling calls on Anthropic: out about three months, as an income stream from its volatility.
- **Malcolm Etheridge:**
  - Needs Anthropic to 10x revenue again to cover about $900B of spending commitments.
  - Values it at about 42x sales on the first three quarters of the year, versus about 100x for SpaceX at its IPO, so he likes it better than SpaceX.
  - Key question is whether it's a commodity model company or a product company (Claude Code, Cowork vs. Copilot, security and medical research tools).
  - Thinks cybersecurity names (Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, Zscaler, Okta) benefit from Claude more than they're hurt by it. Palo Alto was up 5–6% after earnings.
  - Noted Bitcoin is down more than 20% in a month, and that Nvidia has traded in tandem with it.
- **Jim Lebenthal:**
  - Sees a classic correction, now nearing two months in; they usually last two to three months. He thinks it ends with improved sentiment, not a recession or bear market.
  - Said Anthropic's $100B-plus raise brings new outside capital that flows to semis, industrials and power. Hyperscaler spending plans are intact.
  - Said a VIX above 25 is the washout needed, so "get it out of the way."
  - Cited Torsten Slok's claim that two-thirds of recent GDP growth is AI infrastructure.
  - Noted about $250B of Anthropic cash is going into AI buildout, on top of about $1.2T of hyperscaler commitments.
- **Sarat Sethi:**
  - Said it's a great business but he's waiting to see the stock settle, how earnings and competition develop, and whether it has a moat. He sees "no technicals yet."
  - Said Anthropic's enterprise focus beat OpenAI's consumer focus.
  - Likes cash-flow names on sale. He just started buying Boeing (stock down 25%, targeting $10B of free cash flow in 2029). He also likes Waste Connections (15.5x earnings versus an 18x history, raising prices about 5.5% a year).
  - Wants some flush in sentiment before getting more constructive.
- **Apple:** Down about 70 bps. A BTIG study suggests a give-back when Apple is near highs and the S&P is below its 200-day average. One panelist (the transcript doesn't say who) said it's overpriced. They trimmed half a couple of quarters ago and expect to buy it back lower.
- **Market backdrop (Santoli):**
  - The S&P made a new cycle low, below the June lows.
  - Nvidia made a new low, just under $149 a week earlier.
  - A Hunterbrook story alleging AMD China ties pushed AMD lower.
  - Hawkish Fed speak and a strong dollar weighed on financials. Morgan Stanley and Goldman were down a couple of percent.
- **Options Action (Oliver Renick, Cboe):**
  - Pre-IPO perpetual contracts priced Anthropic at $100–105 and the stock opened at $110, so the perps "hit the bullseye."
  - The $80–85B of supply may be a zero-sum drag on the broad market and SpaceX.
  - About $40B of Anthropic stock traded in the first hour, more than SPY and QQQ combined. Options likely start trading Monday. The VIX hit almost 29.
- **Final Trades:**
  - **Jim Lebenthal:** Boeing (a place to start accumulating) and Disney.
  - **Malcolm Etheridge:** AT&T ("market order right there").
  - **Bryn Talkington:** Google, about 30% off its highs, with big Anthropic exposure.
  - **Sarat Sethi:** His pick isn't in the transcript. Earlier in the show he named Boeing and Waste Connections as buys.

Mike Santoli also noted that Anthropic's market cap now exceeds Meta's.
