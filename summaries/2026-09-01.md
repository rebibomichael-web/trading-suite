The September Setup: The Investment Committee's Strategy — September 1, 2026

**Overview:** The Committee debated whether September's historical weakness and momentum's worst quarterly underperformance in 25 years (down ~9-13.7% since June/July) warrant caution, with most panelists arguing the "cavalry" of bearish calls from Wall Street desks is overdone and viewing any pullback as a buying opportunity. Markets sold off sharply intraday after CENTCOM confirmed U.S. strikes on IRGC targets in Iran following an attack on a commercial vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, sending oil toward $90/Brent $94, the 10-year yield to its highest since January 2025, and the Dow down 400+ points; the group also debated the data-center/AI capex backlash (state moratoriums, Wells Fargo/JPMorgan caution vs. Morgan Stanley's "conditional buildout not broad capex destruction" view) ahead of Palo Alto Networks' after-the-bell earnings.

- **Joe Terranova:** Sees sentiment turning too bearish on momentum's rough quarter and thinks that's the "first thing you want to see" before a factor bottoms; watching semis (Micron strength) for a rebound signal. Doesn't think the AI data-center backlash goes away after the midterms, expects headline intensity to weigh on industrials near-term (cites GE Vernova) but takes the other side long-term. Bought **Nutrien (NTR)** for exposure to a spike in ag commodities (potash, wheat, corn, soybeans) tied to a forecasted El Niño event; avoided CF Industries due to Strait of Hormuz volatility risk. Held Amphenol (APH) since Nov 2020 at $31.75, now up ~400%, still likes the mid-30s valuation given its data-center growth. Final trade: **Apple (AAPL)**.
- **Stephanie Link:** Argues the economy is fine (strong JOLTS, ISM up 8 straight months) and rates are rising partly on better growth, not just inflation/deficits; says embrace September volatility rather than time it. Trimmed **Palo Alto Networks** last week after its 145% run since the April low but would buy it back on a post-earnings dip; expects a strong quarter (RPO ~33% growth, total revenue growth ~32%) but cautions the stock historically falls after earnings. Believes AI/data-center spending is a decade-long theme and hyperscalers have simply done a poor job explaining the benefits (jobs, growth, taxes). Final trade: **SLB**, likes its acquisition at 8.5x EBITDA.
- **Josh Brown:** Dismissed seasonal/September-timing trades as "astrology for middle-aged men," noting momentum peaked relative to the S&P on June 22 and has since underperformed by over 16 points — a healthy rotation, not a warning sign, since healthcare and energy have picked up the slack. Best Stocks in the Market segment: raised stop on **Amphenol (APH)** to $145 (rising 200-day) after a record quarter (note: 2-for-1 split tomorrow, adjust levels); on **Dell**, up ~248% since first write-up, sees $500 resistance and a $400 closing-basis stop ahead of tonight's earnings. Final trade: **Apple (AAPL)**, expects it to retake its recent record high into the foldable-phone/iPhone 18 cycle.
- **Jason Snipe:** Likes **Microsoft** after Bank of America reiterated Buy and raised its target to $600 from $500 — cites Azure now a $100B business (+43% y/y) and Copilot adoption at 30 million paid seats; stock up ~27% since earnings reset it. Final trade: **The Linkster** (mentioned briefly — revenue up 40%, record EPS).
- **Data-center/AI capex debate:** Wells Fargo warned of a moratorium risk to the AI trade; PIMCO flagged more state-level moratoriums; Morgan Stanley said federal policy remains supportive and expects a "conditional buildout" rather than capex destruction, with material federal policy risk more likely after 2028. Panelists (Link, Terranova, Snipe) generally agreed hyperscaler spending continues but the industry has a communications/PR problem.
- **Options Action (Oliver Renick):** Notable options activity showed someone closing two large bullish Microsoft call spreads (Dec 2027 expiry, ~$36M total credit across 30,000+ contracts), suggesting some cooling conviction despite the stock's summer rally.
- **Breaking news:** Eamon Javers reported CENTCOM confirmed U.S. strikes on IRGC targets in Iran following an attack on a commercial vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, sending oil and yields higher and stocks lower into the close.
- **Final Trades:** Josh Brown — Apple; Joe Terranova — Apple; Jason Snipe — The Linkster; Stephanie Link — SLB.
