**The State of the AI Trade – 10/9/26**

**Overview:** The panel debated how fragile the AI-driven market is after Thursday's worst day since August, which followed an OpenAI revenue report. They covered concentration risk, hyperscaler capex and private-market funding. The show also covered Apple's iPhone demand report, a hawkish Fed and Humana's star-ratings jump.

- **Steve Weiss:** Worried about circular funding across the AI ecosystem. Spending at some companies far exceeds their revenue, and a failed raise or default could cascade. About $270B of Mag 7 market cap was wiped yesterday. He sees a wide gap between private and public valuations, and said that if the AI companies stop spending, "look out below." He is not calling the end of the cycle, but "if you're not worried... you're whistling by the graveyard." He flagged a treacherous stretch ahead: earnings, the election, the Fed, and possible resumption of military action against Iran. On Apple, he expects strong first-adopter demand for the foldable "Duo" but thinks its camera is deficient, it could cannibalize the iPad, and it costs about $2,000. He noted that iPhone production-cut rumors come up every cycle. He owns UnitedHealth (UNH) and Natera.
- **Jason Snipe:** Concerned about concentration risk and the vulnerability of capex-dependent spending. Baseline S&P earnings growth is about 30%, but it is concentrated in a few companies. He sees more 52-week lows under the surface. He owns Apple and thinks the Duo will do well after the Oct 16 pre-orders. He blamed the staggered launch for the soft demand signals.
- **Stephanie Link:**
  - **AI spending:** Bullish. Hyperscalers are spending about $1T this year and are expected to spend $1.2–1.6T next year, likely at the high end. Nvidia spent $24B on AI R&D, $18B on new architecture, $99B on equity investments (vs. $7B last year) and nearly $400B on supply agreements. She doesn't want to tap the brakes and expects earnings season to confirm strong backlogs and orders.
  - **New buy:** Mirion Technologies. It makes radiation detection and monitoring hardware and software. Nuclear is about 70% of revenue and medical about 30%. About 70% of revenue is recurring, backlog is $1.1B, and it has made 10 acquisitions in six years. The stock is down 29% YTD on slower organic growth. She is adding nuclear exposure after the Constellation–Alphabet deal (890 MW to the PJM grid). She also owns EQT for natural gas.
  - **Other names:** She holds a small, painful position in Las Vegas Sands (LVS), down 44%, at about 12x earnings. She cited market-share gains, buybacks and easy comparisons ahead. She is staying patient on Micron (up 260% YTD), citing demand exceeding supply through 2028 and 24 customer agreements. She sees no fundamental reason to short it.
- **Rob Sechan:** Said the whole market and economy rests on one pillar, the Mag 7 and AI infrastructure (44% of the wealth effect). The real risk is a financing failing, not a change in spending intent. He is neutral on Apple (since July) on valuation and expectations, but says you can't be underweight it long term. He is overweight Meta, which he called very cheap, noting Muse has 2M daily active users.
- **Humana (HUM):** Up 12%+ on 2027 Medicare Advantage star ratings. 95% of members will be in plans rated 4 stars or higher, up from 20% in 2026. Oppenheimer estimates about $3.6B of added revenue in 2028. CVS and Alignment Healthcare fell. Weiss called the turnaround "complete" and said he missed the stock. Link called UNH the best managed-care name at 19x forward earnings (historical 23x), with next week's medical loss ratio and guidance as the key. Snipe's top healthcare name is AbbVie (ABBV).
- **Macro and news:**
  - **Rates and Fed:** The 10-year is at 5.25%. Waller said more hikes are likely needed. Steve Liesman put October hike odds at only 20%, with the market betting on December. He also covered the White House hearing on Fed governor Lisa Cook.
  - **Apple:** Nikkei reported October production cuts of at least 15% for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. The starting price is $1,200, and the Duo goes on sale Oct 23.
  - **Options:** Oliver Renick flagged a roughly $14.5M bearish Micron put spread (2028 expiry).
  - **Macau:** Contessa Brewer reported record visitation in Macau but falling gaming revenue.
- **Final Trades:**
  - Stephanie Link: Wells Fargo (WFC), on net interest margins bottoming
  - Rob Sechan: Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE)
  - Jason Snipe: Arista Networks (ANET)
  - Steve Weiss: UnitedHealth (UNH)
