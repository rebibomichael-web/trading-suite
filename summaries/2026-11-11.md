**CNBC Halftime Report: AI Relief Rally, Nvidia Governance and CoreWeave Earnings (Nov 11, 2025)**

**Overview:** An AI relief rally lifted the Nasdaq about 2% after CoreWeave's earnings beat and its CEO's cancellation of a planned stock-sale program. Nvidia bounced about 5% despite a Baird downgrade that called for a new CEO. The panel debated whether semis and momentum have bottomed, rising-rate worries, energy, and consumer names. The auto-generated transcript blurs some speaker attributions, so a few of the bullets below are best guesses.

- **Nvidia / Baird downgrade (Jim Lebenthal, Joe Terranova):**
  - Baird's Tristan Gerra cut the target to $200 from $500 and was the first on Wall Street to say Jensen Huang may need to be replaced.
  - Lebenthal: a CEO exit isn't his base case or his hope. He thinks $200 is too low and he isn't selling.
  - His numbers: forward P/E just under 12 (a 10-year low), about 75% projected earnings growth, and about $329B of free cash flow over 12 months, more than all but 32 S&P 500 companies.
  - He called it "a governance issue, not a demand issue" and said a CEO exit may already be priced in.
  - Terranova: he bought more NVDA personally. "You can replace the CEO, you can't replace the CUDA architecture," and Nvidia holds about 90% share of advanced chips. He expects an "elongated overhang."
  - Other headlines: Morgan Stanley dropped NVDA from its top-pick list, Sen. Kevin Cramer said Huang should resign, and Reuters reported Chinese universities bought H200s despite the blacklist.
- **CoreWeave and the AI relief rally (Anastasia Amoroso, Terranova):**
  - CoreWeave was up about 25% on a revenue and EBITDA beat, a raise, and rising backlog.
  - Amoroso: operating margin of about 16% eases worries about leverage and cost of capital.
  - She also cited Anthropic's roughly $80B run rate and OpenAI's roughly $50B as proof "these things have to work."
  - Terranova: the cancelled stock sale was the catalyst and the momentum factor is back. The S&P is back in its trading range, and the momentum ETF was up 4.5% versus the equal-weight S&P up only 63 bps.
  - Lebenthal pushed back that equal weight has crushed cap-weighted this year, and that higher rates hurt the broadening trade.
  - Anthropic IPO: prices a week from today. The FT says demand could push the valuation to $1.5T.
- **Oracle, credit and execution (Rob Sechan, likely):**
  - Oracle's 5-year CDS is just under 300 bps, versus a much worse level for CoreWeave.
  - Oracle issued $10B of equity two weeks ago and says it is done raising.
  - The stock "has been looking for an excuse to go higher."
  - Hyperscaler demand is intact (Google Cloud up 100%), so "it is an execution and financing issue, not a demand issue."
- **Semis and AI infrastructure:**
  - Terranova thinks semis have bottomed for the rest of the year, though a rollover in 2027 is possible. He believes hedge-fund and pod leverage has been flushed out.
  - Terranova stopped out of Teradyne Thursday and bought more of the JOT ETF instead. Micron, Marvell and Western Digital were up double digits.
  - Amoroso: semi earnings haven't peaked (about 110% growth expected) and P/Es are at their lowest since 2022. Be selective: about 88% of agentic workflows depend on CPU and memory, not just GPUs. AMAT reports tomorrow.
  - Sechan: the data-center and power builders (Quanta, Nvidia, Micron) are opportunities, but it's a timing issue and not a demand issue.
  - Amoroso: data center vacancy is about 1%, and grid interconnection queues run 5–6 years.
  - Scott: the near-term data center story can't be as bullish post-midterms.
- **Rates and the Fed:**
  - Hawkish Fed-speak (Waller, Bowman) and Goolsbee calling October CPI "borderline disturbing."
  - Goldman sees a third hike by March 2027, and one Fed voice floated 50 bps in December.
  - The 10-year is at 5.0%, up from 4.85%, 30 bps below a month ago.
  - Crude is down about 15% in a week, and one panelist (the transcript doesn't say who) argued a 25 bp hike is more likely than 50.
- **Calls of the Day:**
  - **Energy:** Bernstein downgraded ConocoPhillips, Diamondback and Devon to market perform.
    - Terranova is looking to exit Valero. Oil fell from $110.48 on Oct 20 to $96 after the election, and energy names trade at a 0.97 correlation.
    - On Devon, a panelist said it was never an oil bet but a cost bet at 8x earnings, and it's an easy hold for the buyback and dividend.

  - **AbbVie:** Berenberg target $305, fifth up week in six. Lebenthal cited 25% five-year returns versus 11.5% for the S&P and a beta of 0.33, calling it "a Belgian draft horse."
