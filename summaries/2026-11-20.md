**Halftime Report: Market Sell-Off, Anthropic IPO Volatility and the AI Trade Reset (Nov 20, 2026)**

**Overview:** Leslie Picker guest-hosted for Scott Wapner. Stocks sold off hard, with the Nasdaq turning negative for the year and the Dow and S&P 500 in correction territory. The panel blamed AI-trade doubts, a hawkish Fed, a 10-year yield stuck near 5%, and Anthropic's IPO, which was supposed to clear the air but fell back toward its offer price.

- **Stephanie Link:**
  - **Market view:** Said unknowns plus a hawkish Fed are what the market dislikes, and the Fed "should not nearly be this hawkish." She doesn't think the "blue wave" kills the AI trade. Data-center spend may slow from $7T by 2030 to $2–4T, which is still trillions.
  - **Buying:** Added to Quanta Services this week (power and grid) and has been adding to Meta. She advised legging in and watching the Fed and bond market.
  - **Anthropic:** Wants to own it long term but would wait a couple of weeks for volatility to settle, as she did with SpaceX. She cited revenue up about 1,200% from Q1 last year to Q3 this year ($375M to $17B per quarter) and net retention of 900%. She'd rather buy known names that are being sold hard.
  - **Committee moves:**
    - Added to Zscaler (ZS). It's down 12% on the year versus Palo Alto up 90%, trades at about a 50% discount to its historical EV/sales, and has a long-term ARR goal of $8–10B.
    - Added to UnitedHealth, down 16% in two days on the CMS Medicare Advantage initial ruling. She called it the top company in its industry, with a $50B buyback and a CEO who bought $25M of stock. She expects the cuts to be softened.
  - **Quality on sale:** Pointed to Starbucks and Estee Lauder and said she is "looking for quality on sale." She noted the Fear & Greed index at 15 and the S&P oscillator at -5.
- **Amy Raskin:**
  - **Market view:** Sees a "buyer strike" because holders are overweight winners and don't want to realize gains. She said the AI business model is still unknown, with spending running ahead of revenue, token prices falling, and OpenAI growth slowing. She cited a Howard Marks memo.
  - **Bond market:** Said the worrying sign is the 10-year staying near 5% while the Nasdaq falls.
  - **Mag 7:** Warned that the Mag 7's core monopolies are eroding, citing Google Search. She said AI isn't a natural monopoly and that over-earning could mean "a few hiccups" hurt the group.
  - **Anthropic:** Wants it to succeed but is staying on the sidelines until she understands the long-term business model.
  - **Cyber:** Likes CrowdStrike (trimmed in October, still owns it). She said cybersecurity is "bigger than AI because of AI," and that a roughly $200B+ market cap leaves more room to grow.
- **Kevin Simpson:**
  - **Positioning:** Cautious, with about 12% cash, tight stops and small position sizes. He cited Druckenmiller on capital preservation and noted retail isn't buying the dip for the first time since 2022. He said VIX above 30 makes covered calls attractive, and that the advice today is "focus on math, not emotions."
  - **Macro:** Flagged a Waller comment about a 50bp hike. He said a 25bp hike in December might let the market relax.
  - **Losses:** Was stopped out of Nvidia at a 20% loss, and it has since fallen another $30. Bought UnitedHealth at $390 and was stopped out of half, so he still owns 2%.
  - **New buy:** Started a half-percent position in Texas Instruments. It's about 20% off its highs, industrial sales grew 40%, automotive was up in the teens, and the dividend rose 7% (23rd straight year).
  - **Anthropic and SpaceX:** Doesn't buy IPOs, but would consider Anthropic after pullbacks. He bought SpaceX after its pullback and can sell covered calls against it.
- **Anthropic IPO data:**
  - The stock was around $83–85, down 12–14%, and within cents of its $83 IPO price. It rose 17% on debut and traded as high as $115.
  - The deal raised a total of $119B including the green shoe, and it was the largest offering ever.
  - Options begin trading Monday, and levered ETFs also launch that day.
- **Guests and segments:**
  - **Nick Smith, Renaissance Capital (ETF Edge):** Said the weak trading is "not the icebreaker" other AI IPOs wanted. Scale AI and others may wait until after Thanksgiving.
  - **Oliver Renwick, Cboe:** Expects Anthropic implied volatility of roughly 100–150, higher than any S&P 500 stock. A $90M SMH put trade was the day's biggest, and VIX is above 30 versus 17.5 at the SpaceX debut.
  - **Mike Santoli:** Called it an "all-out purge" concentrated in semis, with S&P 7,000 key support. He compared it to Q4 2018, when a tightening Fed met a strong economy, and expects a snap-back whose character will matter.
  - **Megan Cassella (White House):** Trump said he'll put a "tremendous tax" on companies building data centers overseas, citing Finland (Google, Microsoft, Cerebras) and the Middle East (Amazon). The news came during his post-Asia-trip briefing, with the Jensen Huang resignation fallout also expected to come up.
- **Market stats:** The Nasdaq was down about 3.4–3.8%. The S&P ended the show near 7,025 and the Dow was down only about 0.3% earlier. WTI is around $113, down 27% from its peak. Nvidia is 43% off its highs, Broadcom 35%, Alphabet 32% and Meta 26%.
- **Final Trades:**
  - **Kevin Simpson:** Amazon ("come for the store, stay for the cloud," plus its Anthropic stake).
  - **Amy Raskin:** Verizon (single-digit multiple, 6% dividend yield, defensive).
  - **Stephanie Link:** Target (strong quarter, outcomped Walmart three quarters in a row, transactions up 5%).
