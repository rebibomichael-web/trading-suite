**Markets after Nvidia and Warsh: Your Next Move — 8/31/26**

**Overview:** Stocks slid as rising rates, rising oil, and renewed Iran-U.S. military strikes weighed on sentiment heading into a historically weak September. The committee debated why Nvidia's strong earnings failed to reignite the AI/momentum trade, pointing to a souring political narrative around data centers, a broader rotation into financials/healthcare/energy, and a wave of Merger Monday deals (Aon-KKR/USI, Lilly, SLB, ONEOK) alongside Apple's CEO transition from Tim Cook to John Ternus.

- **Joe Terranova:** Says the "shot of adrenaline" he hoped momentum would get from Nvidia's earnings never came — Micron, Corning, and Marvell all failed to catch a bid too. Sees momentum funds rotating away from high-beta AI into healthcare, energy, and commodities (agriculture prices "on fire"). Argues the market is going back to concentrated MAG7 leadership rather than broadening. Holds Aon and thinks KKR (the seller in the $17B USI deal) is the better trade, banking ~$3.3B. Final trade: Aon.
- **Shannon Saccocia:** Ties the momentum stall to a negative political narrative around data centers ahead of midterms — cites Gavin Baker's Twitter defense of data centers (quote-tweeted by Jensen Huang) and Trump's Truth Social post on the topic. Warns three rate hikes are priced into the market, which could pressure financials/small caps; doesn't see a positive catalyst in the next few weeks but says lack of catalyst doesn't mean investors shouldn't position ahead of earnings season. Final trade: Financials, specifically alternative-asset managers on continued dealmaking.
- **Jenny Harrington:** Sees the pause as healthy consolidation after the market's 13.5% YTD run following years of ~20%+ gains — not a breakdown. Says valuation confusion (Salesforce, Adobe, Caterpillar, GE Vernova) makes it "okay to digest." Likes DocuSign (down 6% YTD, 13x earnings, 10% free-cash-flow yield, double-digit earnings growth) as "de-risked" and hard to replicate with AI tools like ChatGPT, unlike Adobe's graphic-design use case. Final trade: GXO Logistics — 6% FCF yield, 14x earnings, down 6% this quarter despite good earnings.
- **Bryn Talkington:** Cites a bot swarm of 200,000 Chinese accounts spreading data-center misinformation and utility pushback (CenterPoint, PG&E rebates) as real headwinds; flags NVIDIA's 60% accounts-receivable jump this quarter as a red flag. Expects a typical ~10% September–November drawdown into midterms but strong post-midterm returns. Added to Tesla at $360, citing expanding robotaxi/Cybercab presence in Dallas/Austin and improving FSD, with Optimus as a 2027 catalyst. Final trade: GPIQ (equity income ETF, ~1.5% monthly yield-style play... actually cited as a favorite covered-call/income name).
- **Apple leadership transition (Mackenzie Sigalos):** Tim Cook's last day as CEO; John Ternus takes over Sept 1. Cook's farewell memo praised Ternus and touted "culture triumphs over everything." Ternus inherits Mac/iPad price hikes (~20%), likely iPhone price hikes, rising memory costs, decelerating Services growth, and the Siri AI buildout; foldable iPhone debuts next Wednesday at Apple Park. Separately, App Store chief Phil Schiller is moving to an "Apple Fellow" role the same day — shares dipped on the news given ongoing App Store regulatory pressure.
- **Options Action (Oliver Renick):** Tesla options volume 60% above average ahead of Thursday's Cybercab event, ~70% call-skewed; heaviest open interest at the $400 strike. One trader bought $5M in a spread betting on a return to $300 lows post-earnings or a rally to $600 by January 2028.
- **ETF Edge (Dominic Chu w/ Brian Lake, Goldman Sachs AM):** Goldman is acquiring ETF issuer NEOS (following its Innovator deal) to expand into derivative-income products like the NEOS Nasdaq 100 High Income Fund (QQQI), part of an 80%-a-year-growing derivative-income category.
- **Merger Monday:** KKR selling Aon's USI Insurance Services for $17B; Eli Lilly, SLB, and ONEOK all announced deals — SLB's buy targets data-center thermal management, ONEOK's deal touches Permian/LNG infrastructure. Panel expects deal activity to accelerate into midterms on fear of political gridlock after.
- **Final Trades:** Bryn Talkington – GPIQ; Jenny Harrington – GXO; Shannon Saccocia – Financials/alternative managers; Joe Terranova – Aon.
