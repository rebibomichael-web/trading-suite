### [Germany Wants Tesla FSD, Dan Ives Says 80% Merger Odds, Elon Hits $1T](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Tub9GueMi8)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 06

Germany's transport minister, Stefan Schnieder-style pressure aside, is now openly pushing for Tesla FSD Supervised to roll out across Europe quickly and says Germany should consider acting alone if the EU can't agree. This comes after the EU technical committee met but only discussed FSD, with a decision vote now expected in December. The host and guest Joe Bacti read the German shift as a political reaction to the CDU's collapse and a broader turn toward pro-innovation, and they expect more political support for Tesla.

Dan Ives said on CNBC that 2027 could be a "golden year" for Tesla, with robotaxi expansion into more cities in early to mid 2027 and Optimus in the second half. He put the odds of a Tesla–SpaceX merger by the end of 2027 at over 80%.

Analysts agree the AI story matters more than near-term numbers: Goldman (Neutral, $360) says robotaxis, FSD and humanoid robots will move the stock more than Q3 earnings, UBS stays Neutral with a $385 target despite a roughly 5% delivery beat and an energy-deployment miss, and Oppenheimer says FSD could lift both vehicle demand and margins. Bacti expects a strong Q3 report on October 21, citing firm pricing and rising FSD take rates, and thinks robotaxi economics could start appearing in upcoming earnings calls.

Elon Musk's net worth hit about $1.02 trillion after rising $41.5 billion in a day as Tesla and SpaceX shares climbed, and the rest of the video is a discussion of why Musk skips PR and advertising.

### [50x Chip Capacity: Can TSMC Ignore Elon? Jeff Lutz #Tesla](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBg3qxCA_lM)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 06

Jeff Lutz argues that Elon Musk's planned "Terrafab" chip plant, which aims for 50 times today's semiconductor capacity and is being built in the US, has already given Musk leverage before it has produced a single chip. He compares it to Musk's battery push, which prompted the industry to build far more lithium cell supply. ### [Nobody Owns SpaceX Yet… Why?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3QOj-4Hvi2M)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 07

Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas rates SpaceX overweight with a $300 target (the stock is around $174). He says it trades about 40% below mega-cap AI peers on a growth-adjusted basis. He notes that none of the 40 clients in a room he polled owned it. He sees Starship Flight 15 this month, and a successful catch, as potentially the biggest catalyst since the IPO. Q3 earnings land in early November, with consensus around $12B revenue. The two big cloud deals (about $6.7B and $1.1B per month) start in Q4, which one analyst puts at $20–25B.

Leaked conference notes point to 2.3 GW of compute by December and 10 GW or more by late 2027. About 60% is reserved for Musk's own companies (xAI/SpaceX/Tesla, including Grokbot) and 40% for outside customers like Anthropic and Google. Arc Invest estimates each Starship launch adds about $700M of annual revenue, rising toward $1B or more as AI software revenue grows. Elon replied "I agree."

Guest Joe Back is very bullish. He argues compute drives nearly all the value, expects AI satellites at scale in 2028, and sees a run rate of $80–100B by year-end versus $15B last year. He calls the roughly $2.2T valuation cheap and says a ~10x P/E on future profits would still leave big upside. On the other side, the host notes that expiring lockups for early holders could be weighing on the stock. United is putting Starlink on 880 planes by year-end and its whole fleet by 2027, while Delta is waiting on Amazon's Leo.

### [Why Most Investors Lose Money Buying Stocks Right Now!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SZukzE9AQco)
**Mr. FIRED Up Wealth** · Oct 08

The video argues that most retail investors lose money from three mistakes: chasing parabolic "air pocket" stocks out of FOMO (examples: a robotics stock that ran from $16 to $87, Cipher Digital from about $0.38 to $186 and back to $30, Broadcom from $39 to $493), going all in at once instead of dollar-cost averaging in thirds to fifths, and "fear of losing everything," which keeps people from buying quality stocks after they fall (Axon from $885 to about $400, with a DCA range of $300–$400). The host says fundamentals are about 99% of the decision, and that technicals such as 50% retracements, Fibonacci levels and the 200-day moving average only help with entry points. His fix is a "blueprint" portfolio where every dollar has a job: ETFs, growth and dividend stocks, speculative positions capped at 10%, and at least 5% in cash. He prefers buy, hold and monitor over buy-and-forget, and says an S&P 500 index fund beats most individual investors, with options trading being like gambling. He mentions earlier buys of Rocket Lab under $4 and Palantir under $10, and the video ends with a pitch for his Patreon masterclass and Discord. It isn't financial advice, and there are no specific buy calls beyond the Axon range.
