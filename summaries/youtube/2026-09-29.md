### [Can Tesla Really Hit $1,500 by 2027?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fZbl97yK6Og)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 28

Larry Goldberg, a venture investor, argues that Tesla's multi-year pivot from a car company to an embodied-AI and robotics company is still weighing on the stock. He expects a significant re-rating during 2027, though he says he never set the $1,500 target himself. The host's guest on another channel proposed it, and he only called it "not impossible." He expects a NHTSA standard for unsupervised FSD before the next election, with Tesla declaring it unsupervised in 2027. He also expects robotaxi geofencing to disappear by about mid-to-late 2027, and rides at or below $1 per mile, mainly in the Cybercab. Other catalysts he names are semis at a 50,000-per-year run rate, which he says would cover a lot of overhead and add gross margin, and Optimus proof-of-concept trials at customers. For Optimus he expects only about 5,000–10,000 units spread across 5–10 customers, with large-scale deliveries going to Tesla and possibly SpaceX. He notes that Tesla will need to raise roughly $30–50 billion in capital, that robo-trucking is 2–3 years away, and that Terafab is a 2030 story. The host pushes back that the P/E is already about 300 and some robotaxi hopes are priced in, but Goldberg answers that Tesla reached about $450–500 when it was just a car company and that the market will re-rate as these stories deliver.
