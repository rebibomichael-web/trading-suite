### [Elon Just Told China TV Something Huge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyO0gYEIH-c)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 30

Elon told Chinese state TV (China Media Group) that the Tesla CyberCab, which has no steering wheel or pedals and is already running paid rides in Texas, will "soon" launch commercially in Florida and Nevada. He put California at "probably by middle next year" (mid-2027), which is a target and not a sure thing. Texas AV tracker now shows 420 Model Y robotaxis and 126 CyberCabs, up from 69 CyberCabs a few days earlier. On AI, he said Grok 4.7 is a "workhorse" that trails Anthropic's new Opus 5.5, and he expects xAI to catch the frontier sometime in 2027. Grokbot is growing about 100% a month, from roughly 418,000 weekly users, and now links to bank and brokerage accounts for top-tier subscribers.

He also floated 1 billion Optimus robots within 10 years and 100 billion within 20, against a more concrete Bloomberg report of 2,000 to 2,500 robots a week by year-end. He predicted China will solve its chip constraints in two to three years, and he praised Tesla Shanghai, which built about 45% of Tesla's cars in the first half.

Starship Flight 14 reached orbit for the first time on Monday and deployed all 26 Starlink V3 satellites despite one engine out on each stage. The flight was cut to about 3 hours from a planned 10. Flight 15, the first ship catch attempt, is provisionally set for October 19 or later, and Elon has put the odds at 50–60%.

Dates to watch:
- **Friday:** Q3 deliveries, with consensus around 454,000 and bank estimates from 422,000 to 482,000.
- **October 15:** the rescheduled Roadster event.
- **December at the earliest:** the EU vote on supervised FSD, delayed from October 6.
