### [What Comes After the Chip Trade? Tom Lee on AI Agents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hofF_VadCw0)
**Fundstrat** · Jul 30

This one's a webinar transcript (Fundstrat's Tom Lee with Virtuals Protocol co-founder Jansen Tang), not a standard market-call video, so let me summarize accordingly.

This webinar features Fundstrat's Tom Lee and Virtuals Protocol co-founder Jansen Tang discussing why crypto rails (not traditional banking) will power the coming wave of AI-agent commerce, where autonomous agents pay each other for services. Tom Lee compares the setup to past tech cycles: just as cell towers and Apple were the biggest winners in the mobile buildout (not the phone makers), he argues Ethereum and stablecoin/crypto payment rails could be the "second derivative" winners of the AI boom, since agent-to-agent payments need programmability, micropayments, and instant settlement that banks can't offer. Jansen shared traction numbers: Virtuals has generated about $15 billion in trading volume from agent-token launches, roughly $500 million in agent-to-agent transaction volume over the past year, and about $2.5 million in actual profit earned autonomously by trading agents — while cautioning it's not yet true product-market fit. Virtuals monetizes via its VIRTUAL token (used as a base trading pair, capturing ~5-8% of circulating supply locked in liquidity pools), a 1% tax on trading volume (70bps to developers, 30bps to Virtuals), and a 30bps fee on agent-to-agent economic transactions; the company is also building humanoid/robot fleets in Southeast Asia (~40 units) and selling teleoperation data at $50-$300/hour. Tom Lee predicts truly autonomous "agent economy" payment infrastructure is likely under five years away, comparable to how fast stablecoins and perpetual futures went from crypto-native experiments to mainstream products.
