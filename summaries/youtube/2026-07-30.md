### [Tesla Lost $140 Billion. Elon Posted a Roadmap.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EILADPAOtBA)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Jul 29

Tesla shares fell about 14% in two days—roughly $140 billion in value—after Q3 earnings showed record revenue (~$28B, up 26%) but earnings per share of only 33 cents versus the 53 cents expected, plus a 1.4% operating margin, negative free cash flow, and confirmed $25 billion in AI/robot capex. Rather than address the selloff, Elon spent the following four days announcing product news: X Money (peer-to-peer payments) launched for Premium/Premium+ subscribers, Grok 4.6 (1.5 trillion parameters) is slated for around August 7 with Grok 4.7 (2.1 trillion parameters, trained partly on SpaceX's proprietary engineering data) a few weeks after, and a "Giga Grok" tier was teased. He also posted that Tesla's edge is that AI labs have exhausted public internet data, so the next leap depends on proprietary real-world data from Tesla's 1.48 million FSD subscribers (up 56%) and Optimus robots—data no competitor can buy. Separately, Nvidia's Jensen Huang publicly called Tesla's self-driving and Optimus work "revolutionary" and floated a multi-trillion-dollar humanoid-robot market, and Musk didn't deny an eventual SpaceX-Tesla merger tied to the shared "Terafab" chip project (estimated $55-120 billion). The video's takeaway: the numbers are genuinely weak right now, but Musk's public focus signals confidence in the AI/robotics bet paying off later—key things to watch are whether Grok 4.6 ships on schedule, Optimus production actually starts this summer, and robotaxi expansion continues.
