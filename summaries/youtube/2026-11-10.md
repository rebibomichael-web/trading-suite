Peter DiCarlo is broadly bullish on the market and on a range of dip-buy setups. He says $SPY bounced off its Smart Money Zone and thinks it can climb another 1% into the $760–764 area, where he would take some risk off between $760 and $765. He remains bullish as long as the $727 swing low holds. On individual names, he calls $AVGO one of the strongest setups in the market, says $NVDA below $180 "should bounce," and notes $META bounced 10% from its zone. He also flags $CRDO as oversold in a long-term zone, with a traditional swing-high target of $313 within 12 months, and says $NBIS is up 20% from its zone with earnings due this week.

He is more cautious on a few others. He is not long $TSLA, which reclaimed $350 but has only about $420 of upside before major resistance. He is long $RKLB but will cut it if it falls below $44. He wants multiple closes above $160 to confirm $PLTR. For $HIMS, which reports earnings today, he stays bullish while the $22 swing low holds, but he sees a drop toward $17.50 if that level breaks. He also posted deep dives on $ORCL, which he says is near a long-term buy zone, and on $HOOD, which is down 42% from its high.
