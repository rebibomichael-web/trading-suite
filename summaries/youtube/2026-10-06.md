### [Elon Just Got $30 Billion For Something Huge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xl9YnaC3Eqg)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 02

Tesla secured a new $30 billion unsecured credit package: a $20B delayed-draw term loan, an $8B five-year revolver and a $2B 364-day revolver. Tesla says it has drawn none of it and doesn't plan to in 2026. The panel read it as flexible, cheap funding, not a bond. Tesla still holds about $43.5B in cash and liquid assets. The $20B steps down to $10B after 12 months and $5B after 15 months, which suggests Tesla expects to spend on several things over about 18 months, such as chip and solar fabs, Optimus and factory expansion. They also suggested the funding could shrink or be replaced if the proposed merger closes.

The SEC cleared Tesla's voluntary retail voting program, which lets shareholders preset a default vote. It could shorten the 45-day proxy period, but brokers like Robinhood, Schwab and Fidelity still have to build support for it, so it may not be ready for this year's meeting. Retail ownership is estimated at about 15.6% (roughly 600M shares), with institutions buying. The hosts urged avoiding State Street funds, which they say vote against Tesla management.

Elon said the Cybercab is operating in Texas now, is coming "soon" to Florida and Nevada, and should reach California by mid-2026's next year. The panel noted Tesla's slow, safety-first rollout. They also covered Elon's role co-leading the Pentagon's Project Meridian, the White House AI safety accord, Monday's Starship orbital launch, and SpaceX adding about 220,000 GB300 chips a month at Colossus 2.
