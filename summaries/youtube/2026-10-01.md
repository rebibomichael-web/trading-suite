### [Kaspa: Lets Talk About ZKAS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LD-Wx_mfmzA)
**Kaspa Silver** · Sep 30

The video is Kaspa Silver's take on ZKAS (which the speaker calls "ZCAS"), a hard fork of Kaspa that adds mandatory layer-1 privacy. The speaker credits it as the first Kaspa fork to add something Kaspa lacks, but says they see many red flags. They would rather have seen optional privacy built on Kaspa itself. Their main concerns are:
- The anonymous two-person dev team, which they say relies heavily on AI. They cite an AI check that put the whitepaper at 90–95% LLM-generated.
- A 5% cut of block rewards, and full emission in about two years with a permanent tail emission. They say the tail emission does not fix the security-budget problem unless the coin has value.
- A switch to merge mining with Kaspa ASICs that came a day after their own suggestion, which they call unserious. They also say it makes 51% attacks easier, and that the devs got basic Kaspa launch facts wrong (Kaspa launched CPU-first, with a fast emission).
- The name and branding, which they say trade on Kaspa and Zcash and leave people thinking it is a joint effort.

They give the project about 6–12 months. They won't support it, and they note that default-privacy coins other than Monero have rarely survived. They might merge-mine it only to sell the rewards for more KAS, and they say it is a high-risk project, so investors should be careful.
