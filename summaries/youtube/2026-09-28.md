### [WTF Is SpaceX Putting In Orbit?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sWZE3yqLG88)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 27

Big week of SpaceX news: Starship's Ship 41 is set to launch Monday (Sept 29) carrying the first commercial Starlink V3 satellites, with two more flights already lined up for October 19 and October 30 (the latter a full commercial Starlink load) — a pace nobody expected this soon. SpaceX's CFO says AI-driven demand (robots, autonomous cars, always-on connectivity) makes Starship a huge catalyst, and the company is also pushing direct-to-cell phone service, with T-Mobile, KDDI, NTT Docomo, and Telstra confirming Starlink support for the iPhone 18 Pro (though it's unclear if this covers voice or just data). Separately, the FCC is reviewing SpaceX's application for up to 100,000 Gen 3 Starlink satellites, each generating 250 kW and reportedly carrying an Nvidia Vera Rubin chip — Elon suggested this could even scale to a full NVL72 rack, hinting at combining Starlink with orbital AI compute ("Star Mind"), though the guest speculates it's more likely AI just handling onboard tasks like navigation for now. New hardware is also rolling out: a smaller, faster Starlink V5 home terminal (up to 375 Mbps) and a Starlink Mobile V2 satellite with 100x the bandwidth of V1, aimed at enabling native 5G phone service. No specific stock price targets were given; the overall takeaway is that SpaceX's Starlink/Starship ecosystem is scaling and diversifying (consumer internet, mobile, AI compute) much faster than analysts anticipated.

### [Elon Has To Build This Next](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oO2GjAOho3M)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 27

Elon has admitted Grok 4.7 landed third behind Anthropic and OpenAI for agentic coding, walking back an earlier claim it would beat everyone, and Grok has now slipped roughly five times before finally shipping Sept 21. Despite that, Elon says xAI's "second derivative" (rate of improvement) is strong enough to hit "pole position" in about six months, with a Fable/GPT-6-level model expected in 2-3 months (around December) via Grok 4.8 then 4.9. The video's real thesis is compute and distribution rather than raw model quality: xAI now runs roughly 780,000 GPUs across Colossus 1 and 2 (heading toward 1.4M+ by year-end), and notably Anthropic itself pays xAI $1.25B/month to rent Colossus 1 compute through 2029, while Google pays about $920M/month. Grok is also getting baked into everything Elon-adjacent — X (550M monthly users), Grok Bot (agentic assistant now in Tesla cars for $300/month Super Grok subscribers), the $60B Cursor acquisition (used by ~2/3 of Fortune 500), Starlink support, and planned orbital AI satellites (AI1) launching late 2027. The host's prediction/speculation: Elon will eventually need to build a dedicated AI hardware device (wearable/phone-like) to compete with Apple/Google/OpenAI for the "device AI runs on," though a prior report of a prototype phone was called "utterly false" by Elon. Bottom line for Tesla investors: even if Grok stays third but "good enough," Tesla still benefits from a cheap, fast in-car assistant — the risk case to watch is if Grok falls meaningfully behind rather than just trailing.
