### [Is the AI market mirroring the Cisco boom? Lessons from the past.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2NF9pjp_Ts8)
**Fundstrat** · Sep 25

Fundstrat looks at the AI boom through the lens of Cisco's 1990s run: from 1994-97 Cisco rocketed from 80 cents to $9, then got cut 40% by the Asian financial crisis — a drop many assumed marked the top. Instead Cisco kept climbing to $18 a year later, then took another 40% hit from the Long-Term Capital Management blowup, again looking "done." Yet Cisco ultimately ran all the way to $80, a 100x gain from 1994. The takeaway: sharp pullbacks during a genuine structural boom (like Cisco's internet buildout then, AI now) aren't necessarily the top — they're speedbumps — and the analyst believes today's AI market is at a comparable early-to-mid stage of that pattern.

### [How To Make Your Kaspa Node Public](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y_hYjNp1UIc)
**Kaspa Silver** · Sep 25

This video is a tutorial from Kaspa Silver on making a Kaspa full node public so it appears on the network's node map, using his "Kaspa Quickstart" app. The main steps: open the P2P port 16111 on the node itself via a toggle in Kaspa Quickstart, set the node's external/current IP address (with an option to auto-update it if the ISP changes it), then forward port 16111 (TCP) on your home router — using portforward.com for router-specific instructions if needed. Once done, you verify success using amipublic or the node-checker tool at kaspa.stream (More > Nodes), entering your IP and port 16111 to confirm you show up on the public node map. No tickers, prices, or trading calls — this is a technical/how-to video aimed at people already running a synced Kaspa node.
