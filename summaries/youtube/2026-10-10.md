### [TLT Sell Signal Remains Intact | What It Means for Interest Rates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N5EyJgFP3Ww)
**Wicked Stocks** · Oct 07

This Wicked Stocks update says the TLT (the iShares 20-year Treasury ETF) sell signal from the week of July 27 is still in force. The signal came when TLT broke a nearly three-year trend line. TLT is down about 7.6% since then, which matches roughly a 5/8-point rise in long bond yields. The presenter's rule of thumb is that a 15% TLT move equals about a 1.25-point move in yields. They argue the bond market is leading and the Fed is playing catch-up, so rates are likely to keep climbing.

Near-term targets are 75.05 down to 74.41, which would mean roughly another quarter-point rise in yields, possibly by the end of October. Resistance sits at 79.16 now and falls to 78.44 by the week of November 2. That level should cap any bounces.

The bigger target is the low 70s, which would be about a 15–16% drop from the July signal and another half-point rise in yields. That could come within 2–3 months, or within 3–5 weeks if TLT closes below 74.41. They say this could mark a long-term channel bottom lasting through Q1 2027 or beyond.

They advise against aggressively buying the dip and prefer buying on strength after a test of those levels. If TLT instead closes back above the channel bottom, it could rebound to the low-to-mid 80s, where a sell opportunity would appear.
