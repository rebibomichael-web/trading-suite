### [Solana's Co-Founder on the End State of Finance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mIInvdO7e_Y)
**Fundstrat** · Sep 10

Anatoly Yakovenko, Solana's co-founder, lays out his "end state of finance" thesis: he sees blockchain eventually becoming a single unified global state machine where all assets and markets trade in one composable environment, cutting costs and settlement times toward the physical limits of light speed (~100ms). He explains Solana's core tech edge — "proof of history" (inspired by cellular network timing protocols from his Qualcomm days) eliminates block-production collisions, letting Solana run continuously rather than stall like Bitcoin (10-min blocks) or Ethereum (12-sec blocks); Solana has scaled from 1,000 to 2,000 TPS this year with transaction sizes growing from 1KB to 4KB, enabling deeper liquidity routing (up to ~30 markets touched in one transaction via Jupiter) and tighter spreads than centralized venues like Binance. He frames trading/perps as the hardest and most valuable use case, name-checks Hyperliquid as proof smart-contract perps can compete with Binance, and says Solana's own perp platform (Phoenix) is now at technical parity, with real-world-asset tokenization (via Backpack, letting users mint stocks on-chain and transfer to brokerages like Interactive Brokers) as a near-term growth driver. On value capture, he argues SOL's value accrues through priority fees tied to stake, and calls postquantum security (via multi-sig wallets like Squads with rotating quantum-resistant keys) a near-term (~2030) priority rather than an existential worry. He also discusses Solana Mobile: ~150,000 devices shipped, 1,300 apps, with a goal of reaching 1 million active users to make the device business self-sustaining via transaction fees.
