Institutional investors bought roughly $8 billion of Tesla stock last quarter and now own about 55% of shares, even as retail investors have been net sellers, and the overall takeaway is that both robotaxi and Optimus remain on track but are entering a "real deployment" phase rather than a hype phase, with meaningful scaling expected within the next 12 months.
