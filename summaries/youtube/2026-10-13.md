### [Elon Just Made His Wildest SpaceX Claim Yet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dj6WtuhOnE0)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 09

This video is a panel discussion of Tesla and SpaceX bulls covering Elon Musk's latest strategy moves. SpaceX's Grockbot will now route tasks to the best available model, currently Claude Opus 5.5 plus Midjourney and others, in a "land grab" to own the customer. The panel sees this as smart, though they worry about distribution against OpenAI (1.3B users) and Meta. Germany's transport ministry now backs EU approval of Tesla's FSD, rebranded "Tesla Assisted Driving" with a 10% speed-limit cap. Musk also posted that SpaceX could be worth "orders of magnitude" more than the current Earth economy. The panel treated that as a long-term vision, not a price target.
