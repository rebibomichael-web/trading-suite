### [Robotaxi Hiring Surge: New Cities Soon? Cern Basher #Tesla](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L3aKvpIdIog)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 02

Cern Basher says Tesla's new robotaxi supervisor job postings in Atlanta, Chicago, the Boston area, Philadelphia, Michigan, New Jersey and Virginia suggest those markets could launch in roughly 6 to 9 months, and possibly sooner in some. He notes these are almost all markets where Waymo already operates, so Tesla would be following Waymo's path. He says the exact timeline is still unknown, and that Tesla may be taking extra time in Austin on purpose. The idea is to find and fix problems while the network is small, before copying it to other cities. He admits this slower pace frustrates investors who want fast growth now, but argues that fewer problems at scale is better in the long run. No price targets or financial numbers were given.

### [Nobody Expected What Tesla Just Reported](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa70DvVVLBI)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 03

Tesla's Q3 deliveries came in at 486,532, well above the low-end analyst expectations of about 446,000. Production was 464,000, so deliveries beat production and inventory is down to about 11 days. Year to date, deliveries are up roughly 106,000 (about 9%) despite the loss of the US tax credit. Tesla needs only about 311,000 in Q4 to beat 2025 and about 483,000 to make 2025's record year its biggest ever. Energy storage deployed was 13.7 GWh, short of Wall Street's roughly 15 GWh, but the trailing 12 months hit a record 50.2 GWh. Guest Sern Basher, a CFA, credits FSD-driven demand. He also warns that very low inventory could limit Q4 unless Tesla has fixed its battery-pack and production problems, which the Q3 earnings call on October 21 should show.

On robotaxis, 578 vehicles are registered in Texas (420 Model Y and 158 Cybercab). If Tesla keeps up the roughly 17% week-over-week growth in paid miles that Basher estimates, the fleet would need about 1,500 vehicles by the end of November and about 3,000 by the end of December. On SpaceX, Elon Musk and Nvidia's Jensen Huang say each gigawatt of AI compute generates $50–60 billion of annual economic output. Applying that to ARK's projection of 1.1 GW of SpaceX compute at the end of 2025 rising to 67 GW in 2031, Basher estimates SpaceX's buildout could add about 12% to GDP by 2031. Musk says he would be disappointed if SpaceX only reached ARK's numbers. These GDP and revenue figures are the guest's extrapolations and are very speculative.

### [Elon: Cybercab Hits Florida and Nevada Next](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P5OIToZTbHs)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 03
