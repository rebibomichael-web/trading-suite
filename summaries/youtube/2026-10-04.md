The hosts point out that FSD's figure is for supervised driving, so the comparison isn't apples to apples. There are no price targets.

### [Can Tesla Lock FSD to the Speed Limit? Brian White #Tesla](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1AsYM71M8LE)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 01

The video asks whether Tesla could cap FSD at the posted speed limit. Brian White says a hard-coded limit isn't simple, because FSD's behavior is built on "moods" (driving profiles) rather than fixed rules. His suggested fix is to lock FSD into its "Chill" profile, which would probably stay at or below the limit, or to rename the profiles something like "aggressive" and "compliant." The main obstacle is sign reading. No autonomy system reliably reads and responds to speed-limit signs, and it's unclear how much forward-camera resolution that would take, especially when a truck blocks a sign. He treats it as a trade-off Tesla will have to weigh, with no tickers, price targets, or numbers given.
