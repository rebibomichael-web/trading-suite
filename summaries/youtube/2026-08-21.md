### [Full Self Hearing Is a Real Tesla Feature Now](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h-NtDIX3eDk)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Aug 20

Tesla rolled out several updates: vehicle-to-load (V2L) power sharing is now official for Model Y Premium trims with the newer power conversion system, letting owners use an $80 outlet adapter to draw up to 2.4kW to run things like fridges, power tools, or camping gear off the car's battery. Tesla's also using its cars' own AI and speakers to self-diagnose squeaks and rattles by driving over rumble strips leaving the factory (or service centers), a feature Lars Moravy jokingly called "full self-hearing." A certified pre-owned program has launched in the US and Puerto Rico with 150+ point inspections, the remainder of the 8-year battery warranty, and a free month of FSD, though the hosts see it as a modest upgrade over Tesla's already-solid used inventory. Lastly, used Tesla prices are climbing — one example cited was a 2025 Model 3 with 9,000 miles selling for close to the price of a new one, and a base Model 3 going nearly 14% over new-car sticker price, attributed partly to the expired federal EV tax credit pushing up secondhand demand.
