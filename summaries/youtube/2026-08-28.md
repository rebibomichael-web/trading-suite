Elon separately claimed SpaceX and Tesla will beat analyst 5-year growth estimates (SpaceX projected at over 2,000% revenue growth vs. ~119% for Tesla), and both hosts see a Tesla-SpaceX capital/structure merger as industrially logical but speculative.
