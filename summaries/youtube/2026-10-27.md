### [What EVERY Tesla Investors Must Do Right Now!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TmMYfDyEmtI)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 24

The video is a push for Tesla shareholders to back a SpaceX–Tesla merger. Hosts Amy and Alexander Mertz argue that both companies are now AI businesses that each hold half of the stack, so merging them would remove friction from joint ventures like Terafab (chips), Macrohard and compute "megapods". They say it would also combine cash flows and cheapen financing for what could be one of the world's largest companies. They ask shareholders to sign a "merge already" shareholder letter to Tesla's board (SurveyMonkey, linked from Alexander's pinned X post, with a QR code shown). Alexander said it already had about 3,800 signers and 11 million shares after 24 hours, and the letter goes out within a day or so. They also ask holders to vote in the December 3 annual meeting and enroll in the retail program that votes with the board; a later vote can override that enrollment. A merger needs over 50% of outstanding shares, and non-votes and broken votes count as "against."

On terms, they stress that the deal is an exchange ratio of SpaceX shares per Tesla share, not a price. Alexander hopes for 4.2 SpaceX shares per Tesla share, with a $690 price floor. Amy guesses about 3:1. Brad Gerstner reportedly floated 450–550 per share, and SpaceX is expected to book $35–37B of revenue in Q1 2027. Nothing is settled: two special committees are negotiating, and the process runs through a joint announcement, an 8-K, a proxy several weeks later, and a vote they expect in Q1 2027 or later. They say that if the talks fail, trust would erode and SpaceX would build more in-house, leaving Tesla sidelined. They also cite a combined valuation that could eventually reach $100 trillion, against $8.5T and $7.5T for Tesla and SpaceX separately.
