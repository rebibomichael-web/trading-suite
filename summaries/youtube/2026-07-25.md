### [Tesla's Robotaxi Story Just Divided Wall Street](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n1WHbxLFq_w)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Jul 24

Tesla's earnings call split investors: bears point to disappointing auto gross margins, weaker energy margins (down to ~20%), rising expenses, and confusing robotaxi metrics, while bulls note the core business (record 3-year backlog, growing FSD subscriptions) is intact and robotaxi safety looks strong (380,000+ unsupervised miles across six cities, zero notable incidents). The stock fell to around $319 after the report, with SpaceX's implied value near $118, and the panel spent much of the discussion on speculation that Tesla and SpaceX could merge before mid-August, tied to SpaceX's August 4 earnings call, though they stress this is pure theory, not confirmed. Tesla's VP of AI, Ashok, said the robotaxi fleet has grown at double-digit weekly rates and will keep expanding "for the rest of this year," and that instead of city-by-city rollout, Tesla now aims to expand state-by-state, with FSD v15 already 40% merged into the robotaxi fleet's software. Panelists broadly agree communication was muddled — unclear metrics, shifting goalposts, and no clear rollout timeline — which is fueling investor mistrust, but they remain long-term bullish while expecting no major robotaxi scale-up until 2027. One added detail: Tesla is opening a new $30 billion credit line, a notable shift for a company that historically avoided debt.
