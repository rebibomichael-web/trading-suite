### [ARK and Sequoia: People Are Sleeping on Elon Again](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ENmu-qymY54)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Aug 16

ARK's chief futurist and a Sequoia partner who led SpaceX/xAI investments both just argued that Elon's apparent chaos is actually a repeatable pattern: he pours 70% of his focus into whatever the current bottleneck is, fixes it, then abruptly reallocates ("whiplash") — which looked like Starlink and Neuralink "falling apart" before both became huge wins. Applied to xAI, the claim is that Elon deliberately built compute first (Colossus in Memphis, ~100k GPUs live in ~4 months) before focusing on models/product, so xAI trailing OpenAI/Anthropic on coding and revenue today is expected, not a failure — the bet is that the model/product gap closes now that compute is sorted. They also flag Tesla/SpaceX/xAI's new "Terafab" chip project (aiming for a terawatt/year of chip output) as the endgame of owning the full compute supply chain, plus Starlink/direct-to-cell (8M+ users already) as the cash engine funding it all. Caveats worth weighing: both sources are financially biased (Sequoia and ARK are both long these companies), Elon's timelines are chronically unreliable even when direction proves right, and the whole thesis concentrates enormous execution risk in one person's attention with no succession plan. Bottom line for investors: watch factory/compute build-out numbers and where Elon's attention visibly shifts, rather than reacting to "chaos" headlines or specific dates.
