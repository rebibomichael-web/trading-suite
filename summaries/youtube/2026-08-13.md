### [Wall Street Just Found SpaceX’s Hidden Revenue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVA3Pb5Hy98)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Aug 12

Wall Street just spotted a big new revenue stream hiding inside SpaceX: Cursor, the AI coding tool. Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas says Cursor alone could hit $8 billion in revenue by year-end and $33 billion by 2030 — and that estimate doesn't even include Grok, X data, or Starlink — which is why his SpaceX bull case is $600 a share (330% upside from here). Deutsche Bank backs this up, saying SpaceX could hit a $100 billion revenue run-rate this year, driven by $45-50 billion in cloud/data-center leasing deals plus Cursor as a second growth engine (Elon reportedly calls $100 billion just the "baseline"). The guest on the show, Joe Backdy, argues SpaceX's real long-term moat isn't the AI models themselves (which are getting commoditized fast, per Cathy Wood's point that AI costs are collapsing 99.99%/year) but owning the compute — data centers now, space-based compute later — putting SpaceX among a handful of companies that will effectively control the world's AI infrastructure. He's more skeptical that Cursor/Grok alone becomes a multi-trillion-dollar business (thinks more like $100B, not $1T+), arguing enterprises will want AI built in-house rather than outsourced. Bottom line for investors: if you hold SpaceX or Tesla, Cursor's fast growth is upside the market hasn't fully priced in yet.
