### [Tom Lee: The AI Bubble Comparison Is Wrong](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8g1kcbIAdIg)
**Fundstrat** · Aug 17

Fundstrat's Tom Lee, along with Dryden Pence and Jared, argue the AI spending boom is nothing like the dot-com bust because today's Mag 7 companies have real profits, wide moats, and strong cash flow — unlike the shaky fiber/telecom players of the '90s. They compare the ~2-2.5% of GDP being poured into AI infrastructure to the 1850s transcontinental railroad buildout, and note AI spending could exceed the entire Defense Department budget by 2027. On the much-discussed off-balance-sheet financing (the WSJ report flagging up to $1.4-3 trillion in future commitments beyond the headline $600 billion), the panel says this is normal accounting for future obligations, not hidden Enron-style fraud, and reflects companies redeploying free cash flow into their core business instead of just buybacks. Only about 30% of companies report seeing productivity gains from AI so far and just 7% say they've fully implemented it, meaning the real payoff is still ahead as the shift moves from "infrastructure to implementation." The group is unfazed by rising bond yields (10-year at 4.71%) or the $40 trillion national debt, saying markets currently show no alarm, and they don't expect AI-driven productivity gains to cause a spike in unemployment.
