### [Tesla and SpaceX Just Became Much More Intertwined](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9jE7aZcmCVc)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Aug 08

Tesla and SpaceX just unveiled "Terrafab," a joint chip manufacturing megafactory planned for Grimes County, Texas, that Elon Musk says will be the largest building on Earth—over 100 million square feet, 10x the size of Giga Texas, 15x the Pentagon—aiming for more than 1 terawatt of annual compute output, split roughly 25% for Tesla's Optimus robots and 75% for AI "spacecraft" (SpaceX's Starlink-successor compute satellites). Unlike typical chipmakers, Terrafab will vertically integrate logic, memory, and packaging all in one facility, something guest analyst Phil Bell says is essentially combining what Nvidia, TSMC, and Micron do separately into a single plant, because Musk says memory supply is growing only 20%/year while AI demand grows 200%/year. Bell's own demand-growth modeling suggests even one terawatt of compute won't be enough long-term, implying Musk will likely need to build multiple Terrafabs over time. The video frames this as the strongest evidence yet for why Tesla and SpaceX's futures (and a potential merger) are converging, since both companies will depend on chips from the same building, with construction possibly targeted around 2029 and no specific stock price targets given.

### [Elon's New Factory Makes NVIDIA Look Small](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aAv34aqkoZI)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Aug 08

Elon says Tesla and SpaceX are jointly building "Terafab," a $16.8 billion (with up to $119 billion total planned) chip mega-factory on an old coal plant site in Grimes County, Texas, aiming to combine what TSMC, Micron, and Nvidia each do—design, fabrication, memory, and packaging—under one roof. The main building is planned at 2.5 miles long (50x the size of the Pentagon), with a smaller $3 billion pilot fab already under construction at Giga Texas (production targeted for 2027 at the big site). Chips include AI5 (Tesla's FSD/Optimus inference chip, samples this year, volume in 2027), AI6 (tape-out targeted December 2026, mass production second half of 2027, with a $16.5 billion Samsung supply deal already signed), and D3 (a radiation-hardened chip for SpaceX's new "Starmind" satellite compute project, first launches 2027). Elon's rough estimate is that Terafab's output splits about 25% to Tesla Optimus and 75% to AI spacecraft/Starmind. The video's caveat: the "signed" money (Intel/Samsung deals, tax agreements, $16.8B committed) is real, but the terawatt-scale production target and grand valuation claims are unproven ambition, and fabs are notoriously hard to execute (AI5 volume has already slipped once due to Samsung delays). For Tesla investors, the takeaway is that this reduces Tesla's dependence on outside chip suppliers for its Optimus/FSD ramp, and the key dates to watch are AI5 samples this year, AI6 tape-out in December, and the Grimes County groundbreaking expected within months.
