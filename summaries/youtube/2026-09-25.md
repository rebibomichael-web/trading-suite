### [Expert Reveals Best Stocks to Buy Now for the AI Inference Shift](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQhVyoqYcKc)
**Mr. FIRED Up Wealth** · Sep 24

The video is an interview with Ivana Delevska, founder/CIO of Spear Invest (ETF: SPRX, $223M AUM, up 88% in 2023), about where AI investing is shifting next. Her core thesis: the trade is moving from 2023's "training/data center buildout" phase to an inference/agentic-AI phase, which needs far more networking and roughly 1:1 CPU-to-GPU ratios instead of the old 1:12 — favoring names like Astera Labs (her top holding, ~12% of the fund, on CXL memory controllers), plus CPUs and semicap equipment (Lam Research, KLA) as the next capacity buildout. In photonics/optics she likes Lumentum best (tight laser supply, proven execution), is positive but more cautious on Coherent (its 6-inch wafer bet is unproven) and Applied Optoelectronics (higher execution risk from new capacity), and also flags Nokia and Ciena as under-the-radar optics/networking plays. On memory, she holds SanDisk and a small Micron position but warns the cycle is late and highly cyclical — she prefers playing it via semicap equipment instead. She's cautious on "neoclouds" (CoreWeave, Nebius) due to rate-sensitive balance sheets versus hyperscalers, and sees Nvidia's long-term risk as closed-model labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) eventually building their own chips. She's bullish on space (SpaceX, Rocket Lab, calling them the "Coke and Pepsi" of the sector, 5-10 year holds) and overall says despite the recent pullback she's finding many stocks she thinks can double over 3-5 years, staying positive on AI hardware for at least the next two-plus years.
