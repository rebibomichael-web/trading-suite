### [SpaceX Just Solved Starship’s Biggest Bottleneck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RNYpJEBSAnE)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Aug 30

SpaceX just announced Starbase Louisiana, a massive new $100 billion spaceport on 14,000 acres in Vermilion Parish, planned to eventually have over a dozen launch towers and support more than 30 Starship flights per day, making it the largest launch site on Earth; construction starts next year with the first launch targeted for 2029. The site solves the propellant bottleneck — at 1,000+ flights a year you'd need a fuel truck every 86 seconds, so SpaceX will produce oxygen and natural-gas-based propellant on-site instead of trucking it in (Elon confirmed this analysis as "mostly correct"). The deal brings roughly 3,000 direct jobs (Elon suggests it'll be far more, with guest Larry Goldberg estimating way beyond that once ancillary industries follow) at an average salary of $92,600, plus $820 million to Louisiana over 25 years and a $25 million community donation. Including this new site, SpaceX now has 17 Starship pads in development with a combined theoretical capacity of around 15,000 launches per year. The bigger strategic point made by guests: this high launch cadence is what makes Elon's "Starmind" orbital-compute/data-center vision (and eventually Mars/interplanetary logistics, which need ~10 propellant launches per outbound mission) actually feasible — bullish framing for SpaceX (private) as the key enabler of that roadmap, with a related Texas "Terrafab" agreement also cited as evidence multiple states are competing for SpaceX investment. No public-stock tickers or price targets were given since SpaceX is private.
