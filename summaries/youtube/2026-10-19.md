### [Elon Just Admitted SpaceX Can't Do It Alone?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6HUir9o27CY)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 15

Elon Musk spent three days posting about physical bottlenecks like memory chips, power, cooling and construction while the market worried about an AI bubble. Data center debt sold off (CoreWeave's unsecured bonds yield about 10 points over Treasuries), Micron fell four days in a row (about 11.5% in a week), and Broadcom's CEO compared AI investors to kids burning arcade tokens. The cause of the memory drop was China's CXMT saying it wants denser server memory out by the end of 2026. Elon's view is that China may catch up on ordinary phone memory (LP5/LP6), as it did in batteries and solar, but will find the stacked AI memory harder. He also said "even we find it very difficult" to build data centers, and he praised Micron, which JPMorgan says has about $150 billion of contracted backlog and more than 75% of next year's output sold.

His answer to the shortage is Terafab, a chip fab that Tesla and SpaceX will build and run themselves. It starts in Grimes County, Texas, at about $17 billion, and he claims its output will eventually rank in the top 20 of country GDPs (roughly $1 trillion a year). An analyst says it is likely 5 to 10 years from running. The presenter ties the compute demand to Tesla's 319 Austin Cybercabs, the new Grok/X subscription bundle, and Morgan Stanley's view that orbital "distributed inference" is SpaceX's most overlooked growth driver. The FAA also opened a review of up to 456 Starship launches a year, versus a cap of 25 today.

The risks are that Terafab runs years late, that memory costs hurt Tesla's margins in the meantime, and that SpaceX credit default swaps hit a record. SpaceX stock fell more than 4% on Tuesday. The presenter's bottom line is that Tesla and SpaceX hold a lot of scarce assets (batteries, launch and eventually chips) if the shortage lasts another 2 to 3 years. They flag Tesla's earnings call on Wednesday, October 21, as the next thing to watch for memory supply and cost comments.

### [JUST RECORDED: Elon SHOCKS Everyone With Flying Car](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_qTZhaPDZw)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Oct 16

Elon Musk and Tesla's team unveiled the new Roadster at SpaceX's McGregor, Texas test site. They showed it flying with SpaceX-derived cold-gas rocket thrusters, doing hops, a long flight and a "kickflip" while a dummy Starman rode along. The car has two ultra-high-pressure carbon air tanks (about 600 L, roughly 250 kg of air) feeding 10 nozzles, and the rear thrusters make over 80 kN of thrust. A fan system pulls over 2 G of downforce for cornering, and the car is meant to be the only manually driven Tesla. In a live test it hit 0 to 60 mph in 0.81 seconds, and Musk says the production car will beat that. He also claims a half mile in the time other cars take for a quarter, and a top speed above 230 mph. Production is slated for 2028. Musk teased follow-on demos, including a Snake River Canyon jump at roughly Mach 0.5, and said the car may not be legal in every jurisdiction. No financial guidance or price targets were given, so this is mainly a product and hype event for Tesla and SpaceX.

### [All Investor Reactions: What the Roadster Means for Tesla Stock](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SC2xbjFe4-E)
**Matt Pocius on Tesla Stock & Money** · Oct 16

Matt Pocius rounds up investor reactions to Tesla's Roadster event. The common view, from bulls and some analysts, is that it was less a car launch than a recruiting event for top engineers and marketing for an expected Tesla–SpaceX merger. One analyst puts an 80% chance on a merger by the end of next year. Barclays' Dan Levy says a deal could be announced before the annual meeting, which must happen by Dec 6. Tesla reports Q3 results on Oct 20, and investors will watch for merger hints.

Skeptics see little financial impact. Gary Black estimates just 6 cents per share by 2029 and expects "sell the news." JP Morgan stays neutral with a $415 target, and Gordon Johnson keeps his $25 target.

Pocius says Tesla was only down about 2% on a day when the tech sector fell about 4%, and was up about 4% pre-market.

The Roadster comes in three versions:
- **Performance:** road-legal, 0–60 mph in under 1.6 seconds, $50K deposit.
- **Thrust:** race-ready, 0–60 in about 0.9 seconds, $250K deposit.
- **Rocket:** invite-only, 0–60 in under 0.6 seconds.

Production is slated for 2028, and Pocius doubts that date given past delays. He expects Tesla and SpaceX to become one company worth $20 trillion.

He also notes good Cybercab news: zero crashes in the first two weeks, and a fleet that grew about 8x in a month to 300+ cars in Austin.

### [Introudcing KaChat Aiming To Be The Best Social Wallet App On The Kaspa Network](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D89961YQiX8)
**Kaspa Silver** · Oct 16

This video introduces KaChat, a free, open-source social and wallet app for the Kaspa network that the creator has been building for a few months. It's available on iPhone, Android and desktop. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and sent as very cheap on-chain transactions to yourself, so users need no phone number or email, only a Kaspa address. Public broadcast rooms are unencrypted. The app also lets users send KAS inside chats and send voice messages. It includes Kapost (an X-style social feed), online chess where each move is a transaction, and a peer-to-peer marketplace. The creator takes no profit and relies on donations. The ".kachat" domain names go live Friday, October 16th, and their fees go to miners. The creator says network fees are a non-issue at today's low KAS price, and that if fees ever get too high, it would mean Kaspa is winning adoption. The creator is open about the encryption's limits. Quantum computers could break it, but they put that more than 10 years away, and Kaspa developers are already working on quantum-resistant addresses. Encrypted messages could also be harvested now and decrypted later. The rest of the video walks through creating or importing an account. It covers 12- or 24-word seed phrases, an optional passphrase that changes your address (lose it and you lose your funds), and the app's built-in keyboard, which prevents seed-word typos. There are no price targets or trade calls. The channel promotes a Tangem cold wallet (code KaspaSilver for 10% off, with a kickback to the creator).
