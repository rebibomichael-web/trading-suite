### [The Market Is Repricing Tesla’s Advantage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=deMlwU3TASI)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 15

Tesla is quietly winning as rivals retreat: its US EV market share is back above 50% after the EV tax credit expired (which hurt competitors more than Tesla), Zurich became Australia's first insurer to offer cheaper premiums for Tesla FSD supervised-mode drivers (citing 7x fewer collisions over 132 million km driven), and Tesla registered a new subsidiary in Vietnam to sell directly rather than through gray-market imports. Meanwhile Hyundai says it will "overtake" Tesla's self-driving by 2029 by deliberately slowing its rollout to gather data first — a strategy the hosts call self-defeating since autonomy systems need scale/real driver data to improve. T-Mobile's CFO dismissed Starlink Direct-to-Cell as "not a competitive threat at all," citing basic physics limits on signal strength/link budget, though the hosts argue he may be underestimating SpaceX's longer-term ambitions tied to robotics and robotaxis. Volkswagen unveiled a sleek "Mission Efficiency" EV prototype (55kWh battery, 7.5 kWh/100km claimed efficiency) that looks like an answer to Tesla's Cybercab but has no launch date and zero mention of autonomy. Overall takeaway from the hosts: Tesla's multi-year lead in self-driving, insurance economics, and manufacturing efficiency is widening even as legacy automakers talk big without shipping.

### [NEW INTERVIEW: Elon Won't Rule Out A Merger](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ROHzb1r9rdI)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 15

Elon Musk, in a new All-In Summit interview, wouldn't rule out merging Tesla and SpaceX, joking that with "so much collaboration on so many levels," people can "imagine what action one might take" — widely read as a strong merger hint, though guest analyst Joe Consorti thinks a formal announcement this year is unlikely (maybe 20-30% odds) and expects it more realistically in 2027-2028. Musk also detailed the Terafab chip project (a Tesla/SpaceX joint venture in Austin): it's needed both because Taiwan chip supply could be disrupted geopolitically and because existing fabs are maxed out on capacity; an R&D fab is already being built at Giga Texas, with something "useful" expected by end of 2027, though full scale is much further out. Nvidia's Jensen Huang publicly backed the effort, saying "if anybody could do it, he can." Musk also teased the October 1 Tesla Roadster/SpaceX event, saying they need a live audience to prove the demo isn't AI-generated. Bottom line for investors: no confirmed merger yet, Terafab is a long-term infrastructure bet unlikely to move the stock near-term, and the real near-term catalysts remain Optimus, Cybercab, and any concrete merger/shareholder-meeting news.
