### [SpaceX’s Lead is Becoming UNTOUCHABLE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ENbGHy15eFE)
**Brighter with Herbert** · Sep 14

This video from "Brighter with Herbert" makes the case that SpaceX's technological lead is nearly impossible for rivals, including China, to close. Falcon 9 now lands autonomously (600+ times) using refined flight software rather than generative AI, and the Raptor engine has been radically simplified across three generations by aggressively deleting parts. On scale, one Starship test flight carrying ~20-40 V3 Starlinks could deliver 25-55% of China's entire 2026 orbital payload total, and if Elon's 2030 target of 30+ daily Starship launches happens, SpaceX would launch more mass every 5 days than humanity has launched in all of history combined. SpaceX also just acquired turbine maker APR Energy to make its own turbine blades/vanes in-house, which ARK Invest's analyst says could pull forward AI-compute power buildout by about 18 months (from ~2GW toward ~10GW by end of next year); SpaceX and Tesla are also separately targeting 200GW of combined solar manufacturing capacity by 2029. The video closes on a political jab: Elon rebutted Senator Bernie Sanders' wealth criticism by noting his net worth is mostly illiquid stock, not cash, and that shareholder value rises only as his companies do useful things.
