# The Market Bull Run Continues: The Committee's Next Move — August 11, 2026

**Overview:** Stocks sat in a wait-and-see tape ahead of Thursday's CPI print, with yields a touch lower and oil off its highs. The Committee doubled down on bullishness — pointing to blowout earnings revisions, resilient positioning, and NVIDIA's massive new compute-financing consortium — while also digesting a rough day for retail/apparel names, Alphabet's pullback, and news of Anthropic's approaching IPO and another OpenAI executive departure.

- **Joe Terranova**: Watches the S&P equal-weight index (new high Friday) as his tell for market health and stays bullish until it breaks down; on NVIDIA's financing deal, argues compute is becoming collateral for an eventual futures market "like oil," and pushed back on compute-commoditization fears; holds RTX and General Dynamics on the $1.5T defense budget; sold Fifth Third to add to other financial names (Belsky disagreed, saying Comerica integration makes Fifth Third worth keeping); final trade: **Airbnb (ABNB)**.
- **Brian Belsky (BMO strategist, guest)**: Says the "Yeah, But Bull" skepticism is missing the steepest earnings-revision path since 2000; swapped Qualcomm for Truist in the dividend-growth portfolio, favoring financials' yield/growth over chips; called Alphabet's pullback (Goldman PT $435) a buying opportunity and one of "five stocks in the US you should absolutely own"; long-time Expedia holder (bought pre-earnings in small/mid-cap and value portfolios), thinks it's fundamentally better than Booking; final trade: **Gilead (GILD)**.
- **Rob Sechan (Robbie)**: Notes sentiment is stretched but institutional positioning (37th percentile) is not, citing 15% Q2 revenue growth (fastest since 2021) and an 88% beat rate; argues the payoff from being bearish into a hot CPI is small (5-10% downside) since nothing points to a >20% drawdown; passed on Anthropic's private round, says large "engineered" IPOs with lockups (SpaceX, Meta) need time for price discovery; owns SpaceX from the private side, wants to see Starlink growth and disciplined capex; final trade: **KLA Corp (KLAC)**.
- **Josh Brown**: Says don't bet against Jensen Huang — calls NVIDIA's $500B third-party financing consortium (Goldman, Apollo, Blackstone, Brookfield, KKR, BlackRock) a "brilliant," de-risking move that caps NVIDIA's own exposure at 25%; dismissed the retail/apparel selloff (On Holdings, Under Armour, Nike, Dick's) as a group to avoid entirely right now; his Best Stock in the Market pick is **Expedia** — a "textbook breakout" with Q2 revenue +14% to $4.3B on $34B gross bookings and $900M returned YTD of a $5B buyback authorization; pivot at 290 for traders, 260 (rising 50-day) for investors; final trade: **Snowflake (SNOW)**.
- **Featured segments**: Goldman's Tony Pasquariello (via Closing Bell clip) laid out the bull case — durable economy, superb earnings growth, favorable fund flows, $1T of AI capex. Kate Rooney reported Anthropic is meeting pre-IPO investors (possible October listing) amid questions about its ~$47B+ revenue run rate against cheaper Chinese open-source models, and broke news that OpenAI's Brad Lightcap (COO turned special-projects lead, employee since 2018) is departing to start something new, with power consolidating under Greg Brockman ahead of an IPO. Mike Santoli said the tape looks "cleaner" after last week's forced deleveraging/hedge-fund blowup, with the 5-day put/call ratio at rock bottom showing fast money re-risking. Oliver Renick flagged the day's biggest options trade — a $70M roll of RTX 125-strike calls from December to February expiry, a stock-replacement play with volume 5x average.

**Final Trades**: Josh Brown – Snowflake (SNOW); Rob Sechan – KLA Corp (KLAC); Brian Belsky – Gilead (GILD); Joe Terranova – Airbnb (ABNB).
